# Workshop Facilitation: Cheatsheet

## Numbers and Rules at a Glance

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Group size | 3-10 for exercises; ~7 retros; ~8 for outcome workshops (each extra person ~ +1 h of discussion); breakouts of ~5 beyond 30 |
| Focused work/day | ~3 h; best work in the morning |
| Pre-brief | 2-7 days before (not more than a week); reminder the day before |
| Silent writing | 3-5 min goals/questions; 5-8 min ideas/problems |
| Read-out | 30-60 s per note, no explanation |
| Lightning demos | 15-30 min solo; 1-2 min per presentation |
| Explanation | What / Why / How + example; repeat (about half, three quarters, ~95% understand after 1, 2, 3 passes) |
| Dots | size to options; use all dots; silent and simultaneous |
| Key questions | max 3 ("Can we..."); goals "In two years..." |
| Remote | short blocks, 5 min break in 2 h, pre-filled board, homework in working hours |
| Test users | ~5 per round |
| Repertoire | ~10 exercises, swap ~half per session |

## Agenda Templates

### A. One-hour problem jam (Lightning Decision Jam, 6-8 people)
| Min | Step |
|---|---|
| 0-5 | Roles, silence rule, example |
| 5-12 | Silent problems (7 min) |
| 12-22 | Post and explain (30 s each); silent vote 2 dots |
| 22-27 | Reframe top problems as HMW |
| 27-35 | Silent solutions (7 min) |
| 35-40 | Silent vote 6 dots |
| 40-50 | Effort-impact matrix ("higher/lower") |
| 50-60 | Sweet spot -> next actions, owners |

### B. Ten-minute brainstorm (10 for 10)
HMW posted -> 5 min silent ideas -> 1 min pick 10 -> post all at once -> 3 min vote (10 dots) -> keep top 10.

### C. Half-day alignment (goal + questions + decision, ~3 h)
Expectations notes (15) -> long-term goal note-and-vote (30-40) -> key questions "Can we" note-and-vote (30) -> break -> lightning demos (30-45) -> concepts as hypotheses (30) -> heat map + decider pick (20) -> owners and dates (15).

### D. One-day collaboration workshop (skeleton)
| Phase | Content | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Collect | sailboat (positives, blockers, vote) | ~45 min |
| Choose | vote challenges, make winner an HMW | collect + choose ~1 h total |
| Create | lightning demos ~45 min, then 10 for 10 | ~1.5 h |
| Commit | effort-impact action board; experiments with owner and timeline | ~1 h |
Add breaks and an energiser after lunch; keep to ~3 h focused work.

### E. Strategy workshop (1-2 days)
Welcome, roles, rules -> goal (15 min solo) -> metrics (10 min) -> lightning demos (25) -> concepts as hypotheses (30) -> day 2 heat map, straw poll, decider picks 3-4 -> roadmap by horizon (30 days / 3-9 months / longer) with owner and check-in date.

### F. Growth experiment workshop (90-120 min remote)
HMW on every board section -> demos 15 -> heat map 10 -> choose and pitch 3 + 1 each -> experiment template 15 -> vote 3 dots + decider 5 -> top 3 copied, follow-up scheduled.

### G. Design sprint (four days, compressed)
Day 1: interviews + HMW, goal, questions, map, lightning demos, four-part sketch. Day 2: heat map, critique, straw poll, super vote, test flow, storyboard. Day 3: prototype. Day 4: five user tests.

### H. Retrospective (sailboat, ~90 min full / ~30 min core)
Rules + example -> positives (3 min) -> blockers silent (8) -> theme (optional) -> vote 8 dots (6) -> prioritise: top, 3+ votes, park 1-2 -> actions.

### I. Two-hour design-thinking introduction
Icebreaker -> 45 s drawing demo -> stages -> empathy map (3 + 7 min) -> POV and HMW -> Crazy Eights -> prototype 20 min -> test.

| Goal | Exercise | Time | Group | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Align on a direction | Long-term goal + key questions | 45-60 min | 5-10 | goal + 3 questions |
| Set objectives | OKR workshop | ~2 h | 3-10 | 3 objectives, 3 key results each |
| Surface blockers | Sailboat | 30-90 min | ~7 | ranked blockers |
| Solve a problem fast | Lightning Decision Jam | ~1 h | 4-8 | actions with owners |
| Get many ideas, ranked | 10 for 10 | 10 min | 4-8 | top 10 |
| Borrow from others | Lightning demos | 30-45 min | 3-10 | wall of big ideas |
| Quantity of visual ideas | Crazy Eights | 8 min + 3 refine | any | sketches |
| Choose among concepts | Heat map, speed critique, straw poll, super vote | 45-60 min | up to ~10 + decider | one concept |

| Understand users | Empathy map | 10 min | small groups | persona view |
| Large crowd | Parallel breakouts ~5 | per exercise | 30+ | spokesperson reports |
| Tame a chaotic meeting | 4 Cs | any | any | owned actions |
| Reset energy | Energiser (find something blue; guess whose fun fact) | 2-3 min | any | |

## Ice-Breakers and Energisers
My first job (name, first job, one lesson) · 45-second drawing of a neighbour · find something blue and touch it · guess who wrote which fun fact · short walk · high-five after milestones. Skip forced ice-breakers with senior leaders in high-stakes rooms.

## Voting Quick Table
Goal 1 dot (decider apart) · Questions 3 · Problems 2 · Solutions 6 · 10-for-10: 10 · Retro 8 · Experiments 3 · Concepts many/unlimited · Decider: no vote, or extra dots, or super vote (announce which).

## Hand-offs
Research design, flows, screens -> `ux-ui-expert` · Feedback to a team, idea quality -> `creative-director` · Visual direction -> `art-director` · Slides for a readout -> `presentation-design` · Price negotiation -> `pricing-and-sales-negotiation` · Strategy content -> `marketing-mba`.
