# Glossary: Workshop Facilitation

**4 Cs** — Collect, Choose, Create, Commit; the phase skeleton of a workshop or a meeting section (ch01)

**Action board** — effort-impact matrix whose winners become owned experiments (ch06, ch10)

**Anchor word** — one word a facilitator picks to carry into the session as part of priming (ch02)

**Art museum** — silent walk-through of concepts with dots and sticky comments, no talking (ch06)

**Big idea (lightning demo)** — one short sentence describing what an example does that is worth borrowing (ch06)

**Buffer** — extra time added for large groups or unknown pacing (ch01)

**Collect / Choose / Create / Commit** — see 4 Cs

**Creative confidence demo** — a short drawing exercise (45 seconds) to lower fear of sketching (ch01, ch08)

**Crazy Eights** — fold a sheet into eight frames and sketch eight variants, 1 min each (ch06)

**Decider** — the one accountable person who makes the final call after votes (ch04)

**Diverge / converge** — generate many options, then narrow to a decision (ch01)

**Dot vote** — silent voting with sticker dots; count sized to options (ch06)

**Effort-impact matrix** — 2x2 placing solutions; high impact, low effort is the sweet spot (ch06)

**Emotion curve** — expected mood over the day, shown at the start so dips read as normal (ch01)

**Empathy map** — Say, Think, Do, Feel (plus Pains, Gains, Jobs) for a persona (ch08)

**Energiser** — 2-3 minute active activity to raise energy (ch03)

**Experiment template** — name, hypothesis, action steps, success criteria (ch06)

**Facilitator** — process owner, neutral on content (ch02)

**Four-part sketch** — notes, ideas, Crazy Eights, solution sketch (ch06)

**Groan zone / messy middle** — middle of a group decision with tangents, falling energy and dominant voices (ch01)

**Heat map** — silent dot voting on anything interesting across displayed material (ch06)

**How might we (HMW)** — open question reframing a problem as an opportunity (ch06)

**Key question** — yes/no "Can we..." risk statement that makes a goal testable (ch05)

**Key result** — measurable outcome of an objective, time-bound and a stretch (ch05)

**Lightning Decision Jam** — about one hour mostly silent sequence from problems to ranked actions (ch06)

**Lightning demo** — silent research of analogous solutions from any industry, presented briefly (ch06)

**Long-term goal** — optimistic best-case statement for roughly two years out (ch05)

**Map** — simple journey map (Discover, Learn, Use, Goal) used to locate the focus (ch07)

**Note and vote** — write silently, vote silently, then decider chooses (ch03, ch05)

**Objective** — short, concrete, action-oriented, inspiring goal (ch05)

**Parking lot** — visible list of deferred topics with owner and return time (ch04)

**Priming** — deliberate pre-session routine for the facilitator's own state (ch02)

**Sailboat** — retro exercise: sail = what helps, anchor = what holds back (ch05)

**Solution sketch** — anonymous three-panel self-explanatory concept poster (ch06)

**Speed critique** — facilitator summarises concepts around where the dots cluster (ch06)

**Sprint question** — a "Can we..." risk chosen to be answered by the sprint (ch05, ch07)

**Storyboard** — about eight cells showing the test flow screen by screen (ch07)

**Straw poll** — silent vote with a short pitch, informing the decider (ch06)

**Super vote** — the decider's own timeboxed choice (ch06)

**Together alone** — individual silent work toward one shared goal (ch03)

**User test flow** — six action steps, voted, that become the storyboard row (ch07)

**What / Why / How** — formula for explaining any exercise (ch03)

**Wizard of Oz prototype** — facade whose back end is operated by a human (ch07)
