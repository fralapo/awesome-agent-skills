# Patterns

Recurring situation -> move. Each entry: When / Move / Cost / Not when. Chapter in brackets.

## 1. "One cause" claim
**When**: a source or speaker names one villain.
**Move**: split into underlying, amplifier, trigger; ask for evidence of each; ask who survived the same shock. [ch01, ch02]
**Cost**: a less tidy answer.
**Not when**: a single dated catastrophe with no earlier strain.

## 2. Burn layer attributed to a named enemy
**When**: ash and arrowheads, a famous raider offered.
**Move**: look for texts naming attackers, diagnostic weapons or ships; consider neighbours, revolt, accident. [ch01, ch05]
**Cost**: leaves the cause open.
**Not when**: attackers are named by the victims' own letters.

## 3. Silence before the end
**When**: monuments, records or messages simply stop.
**Move**: list readings (no crisis felt, taboo, skill lost, orderly leaving, record lost) and what each predicts. [ch01]
**Cost**: unresolved.
**Not when**: the pattern shows interruption (half-carved monument, buried hoards).

## 4. Climate as sole cause
**When**: a paper or story blames a drought or cooling.
**Move**: check proxy scale and dating; ask whether comparable shocks were survived before; ask what made the society vulnerable. [ch03]
**Cost**: lessens the clean external explanation.
**Not when**: the signal is large, local, well dated and the social record shows no other change.

## 5. "They failed to adapt"
**When**: a story blames cultural stubbornness.
**Move**: look for evidence of what they did change (diet isotopes, techniques); ask what could not be copied. [ch03]
**Cost**: weakens a moral story.
**Not when**: evidence shows refusal despite available means.

## 6. Essential import
**When**: a system runs on an input it cannot make.
**Move**: list sources, routes, hubs, buyers; estimate time to substitute. [ch04]
**Cost**: duplicate supply.
**Not when**: the input is replaceable at low cost.

## 7. Alliance as insurance
**When**: states rely on partners for help.
**Move**: ask whether partners suffer the same shock and where their forces are. [ch04]
**Cost**: autonomy.
**Not when**: partners are in independent zones.

## 8. Single-export economy
**When**: a colony or city lives from one product.
**Move**: watch the buyer's market and substitutes; plan a second income. [ch04]
**Cost**: lower peak profit.
**Not when**: the export is a minor share.

## 9. Maintenance-heavy infrastructure
**When**: large irrigation or flood works.
**Move**: estimate labour needed; find the minimum viable population; check recent repairs and signs of neglect. [ch02, ch03]
**Cost**: ongoing budget.
**Not when**: modular, cheap to repair.

## 10. Legitimacy tied to outcomes
**When**: ruler's authority depends on harvests, victories or sacred role.
**Move**: track indicators (monument rate, titles, unfinished work, religion changes). [ch05]
**Cost**: ambiguous signals.
**Not when**: authority is procedural.

## 11. Competing extraction
**When**: local elites rewarded for sending revenue upward.
**Move**: ask who benefits from clearing land; look for land degradation. [ch05]
**Cost**: slower expansion.
**Not when**: caps exist.

## 12. Technology shift offered as cause
**When**: iron, gunpowder or a new tool is proposed.
**Move**: check whether the tool appears before the break; ask what the old monopoly held. [ch05]
**Cost**: dismisses an attractive story.
**Not when**: dated finds precede destruction.

## 13. "Disappeared without a trace"
**When**: a society seems to vanish.
**Move**: search for continuity: squatters, reuse, relocation, later names. [ch02]
**Cost**: lessens drama.
**Not when**: mass deaths are shown.

## 14. Modern lesson requested
**When**: "what does this teach us about today?"
**Move**: translate mechanism, match variables, list disanalogies, give a test, say what history cannot tell. [ch07]
**Cost**: qualified answer.
**Not when**: only a metaphor is wanted; label it.

## 15. Epic or saga as source
**When**: a poem or saga is offered as proof.
**Move**: use for memory and attitude; find a physical check. [ch01]
**Cost**: loses colour.
**Not when**: the ground corroborates a detail.

## 16. Population figure quoted
**When**: "a million people" or "half a million".
**Move**: ask the basis; give a range; prefer trend indicators. [ch01]
**Cost**: less quotable.
**Not when**: discrete counts of finds.

## 17. Unknown case
**When**: a society not covered by a sheet.
**Move**: run the ten-step checklist and state that no sheet exists. [ch08]
**Cost**: slower.
**Not when**: a sheet exists; start from it.
