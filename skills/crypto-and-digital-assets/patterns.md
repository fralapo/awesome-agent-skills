# Patterns

Recurring situation -> move. Each entry: When / Move / Cost / Not when. Chapter in brackets. Entries marked (position) are contested. Educational, not advice.

## 1. "My balance is safe because it shows on screen"
**When**: assets sit with a venue or platform.
**Move**: ask who holds the keys, whether assets are segregated, what reserves and liabilities are shown, and what happens in insolvency. [ch03, ch05]
**Cost**: friction and fewer conveniences.
**Not when**: segregated, audited and regulated custody is verifiably in place.

## 2. "This yield is high and safe"
**When**: an interface advertises a rate above the risk-free rate.
**Move**: trace the source of the return: fees, incentive tokens, lending to a counterparty, leverage. Add platform risk. [ch05, ch07]
**Cost**: fewer products qualify.
**Not when**: revenue is a transparent fee that can be checked independently.

## 3. "The halving will do X" (position)
**When**: a cycle narrative is used to justify action.
**Move**: count independent events, test with non-overlapping windows, control for time, compare the direct supply effect with daily volume. [ch02, ch08]
**Cost**: no neat story.
**Not when**: a large, abrupt supply change is at issue.

## 4. Correlation shown as proof
**When**: a chart pairs price with supply, rates or search interest.
**Move**: detrend, add time as a regressor, check sign stability across subperiods, exclude variables that embed price. [ch08]
**Cost**: weaker headlines.
**Not when**: the mechanism is mechanical and measured directly.

## 5. Leveraged exposure
**When**: borrowing against a volatile asset to buy more.
**Move**: compute the liquidation price and maximum drawdown history; ask what forced selling does at the low. [ch07, ch08]
**Cost**: lower upside.
**Not when**: collateral is stable and the loan small.

## 6. Hedging a volatile holding
**When**: shorts or puts to cover a fall.
**Move**: price the premium and the forgone gain, including liquidation probability for margin shorts; compare with simply holding less. [ch08]
**Cost**: exposure remains.
**Not when**: genuine information exists on a near-term event.

## 7. Secret recovery that nobody tested
**When**: a phrase or passphrase has been written once.
**Move**: rehearse on a test network, recreate the first address in independent software, keep several copies in different places, and document succession. [ch03]
**Cost**: time and a risk of exposure during testing.
**Not when**: the amount is trivial.

## 8. Choosing between a wrapper and the asset (position)
**When**: deciding how to hold exposure.
**Move**: list the failure modes of each (key loss, issuer insolvency, custodian, cost) and the use needed (on-chain use, succession, tax). [ch06]
**Cost**: no single answer.
**Not when**: only one is available to the holder.

## 9. Unsolicited opportunity
**When**: an unknown person or ad offers a profitable investment.
**Move**: do not engage; verify through a channel you opened; search the name plus "scam"; test small withdrawal; refuse urgency. [ch10]
**Cost**: occasional missed legitimate offers.
**Not when**: you initiated contact with a registered intermediary.

## 10. "I need to pay a fee to release my funds"
**When**: withdrawal blocked after gains.
**Move**: stop paying; preserve evidence; report; ignore "recovery" offers asking advance fees. [ch10]
**Cost**: accepting the loss.
**Not when**: never for prepaid "taxes".

## 11. Unclear tax treatment
**When**: a swap, reward or product does not fit a category.
**Move**: classify by event type, document the reasoning, keep lots, check current official guidance, take qualified advice. [ch09]
**Cost**: time and fees.
**Not when**: everything is held through an intermediary acting as withholding agent.

## 12. Complexity as the risk
**When**: the setup is so elaborate that the owner cannot run it unaided.
**Move**: simplify to a design you can restore from a written procedure; test it. [ch03, ch04]
**Cost**: fewer protections.
**Not when**: holdings justify professional help.

## 13. A market metric as a timing call
**When**: a funding rate, an open-interest change, a liquidation map or an on-chain band is read as a sign that price will move.
**Move**: write the definition, source, venue and lag; check the reading against an observed event series; state the falsifier before using it. [ch11]
**Cost**: less immediate answers.
**Not when**: the metric is used only to describe current positioning, with no timing claim.
