# Patterns

Recurring situation -> move. Each entry: When / Move / Cost / Not when. Chapter in brackets.

## 1. "The rule is broken" vs "the rule works as designed"
**When**: a result looks perverse (popular-vote loser wins, two parties only).
**Move**: state what the rule was designed to do, what it rewards, and who gains from change; compare with a country with another rule. [ch02]
**Cost**: separates the mechanism from the complaint; less rhetorical punch.
**Not when**: the design goal itself is the question (then it is a value debate).

## 2. Money in politics claim
**When**: a donor or group is said to "buy" outcomes.
**Move**: split into votes, agenda, access and networking; ask for evidence of each; check disclosure and coordination rules. [ch02.8]
**Cost**: less satisfying than a single story.
**Not when**: a documented quid pro quo exists.

## 3. Price rise, which kind?
**When**: a headline says prices are rising.
**Move**: general or specific; demand or supply; fundamentals or speculation; real or nominal; which basket. [ch04.1 to ch04.3]
**Cost**: more questions.
**Not when**: a currency collapse with printing is evident.

## 4. Crisis search for a villain
**When**: a financial crash prompts one culprit.
**Move**: map incentives along the chain (originators, raters, buyers, insurers, regulators, central bank); ask who bore the risk; ask what failed in the safety net. [ch04.4 to ch04.6]
**Cost**: diffuse blame.
**Not when**: fraud is proven.

## 5. "Artificial borders explain it"
**When**: a state's troubles are put down to colonial borders.
**Move**: check counter-cases (clumsy borders, stable states; homogeneous, failed ones); add state design, stakes of office, ethnic politicisation. [ch05.4, ch09.4]
**Cost**: weakens a neat story.
**Not when**: the dispute is itself about the line.

## 6. Resource wealth, poor country
**When**: a resource-rich state is poor.
**Move**: ask who captures rents, what institutions exist, what the revenue dependence is. [ch05.6, ch07.6]
**Cost**: no single answer.
**Not when**: the resource is newly found and not yet developed.

## 7. Strait or canal shock
**When**: shipping through a narrow route is disrupted.
**Move**: width, flow share, bypass capacity, cost of the long route, who can interdict, linked chokepoints. [ch07.1 to ch07.3]
**Cost**: a more detailed model.
**Not when**: the shock is short and priced in.

## 8. "Geography decided it"
**When**: a story presents geography as destiny.
**Move**: name the constraint, the technology that changed it, the choices left open, and non-geographic causes. [ch06]
**Cost**: lessens the elegance.
**Not when**: a physical limit is absolute (water, desert, high mountains) in the question asked.

## 9. A date for war
**When**: a countdown to conflict circulates.
**Move**: translate into observable indicators (mobilisation, logistics, sanctions preparation, financial moves); ask what waiting gains each side; label as position. [ch08.7]
**Cost**: no headline.
**Not when**: the date is set by an actor's own calendar (expiry, election).

## 10. Rising power, established power
**When**: analysts invoke an ancient trap.
**Move**: check structure (shift in relative power), fear, alliance obligations, nuclear and economic ties; note that war is more likely, not certain. [ch08.3]
**Cost**: a hedged conclusion.
**Not when**: one side has already stated a war aim.

## 11. A single national "character"
**When**: a nation is blamed or praised as one actor.
**Move**: disaggregate: leaders, factions, institutions, public opinion; compare with other states with the same trait. [ch08.4]
**Cost**: less rhetorical force.
**Not when**: the question is about a state's formal decision.

## 12. Heroic or villain narrative
**When**: a story gives a person or group a pure motive.
**Move**: list incentives, independent evidence, who tells the story and when. [ch10.7, ch10.9]
**Cost**: undermines inspiration.
**Not when**: independent sources confirm the motive.

## 13. Hidden-hand explanation
**When**: a secret network is offered as the cause of many events.
**Move**: separate documented membership from claimed causation; name the gap; use inquiry and court findings. [ch10.4]
**Cost**: leaves open questions.
**Not when**: judicial findings establish the link.

## 14. Label confusion
**When**: "capitalism", "socialism", "communism", "liberal", "empire" used loosely.
**Move**: define the mechanism meant (ownership, planning, welfare, party-state, settlement, extraction). [ch03, ch09, ch10]
**Cost**: slower debate.
**Not when**: all parties already share the definition.

## 15. Doing analysis on a live dispute
**When**: the user asks about a present-day conflict.
**Move**: use the checklist; describe each side's stated position and constraints with dates; do not rank parties; flag unverified recent claims. [ch11, ch01.5]
**Cost**: no verdict.
**Not when**: the user asks for a factual summary of a documented ruling or treaty.
