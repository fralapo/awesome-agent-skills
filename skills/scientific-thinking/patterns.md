# Patterns

Recurring situation -> move. Each entry: When / Move / Cost / Not when. Chapter in brackets. Entries marked (position) are practitioner judgment, not evidence.

## 1. A claim that fits every outcome
**When**: a theory explains success and failure alike.
**Move**: ask for the observation that would make the speaker drop it; if none, treat as story. [ch01]
**Cost**: awkwardness, slower agreement.
**Not when**: values, definitions or math statements.

## 2. "We tried it on the worst cases and they got better"
**When**: a program targets the worst performers, sites, patients.
**Move**: add a comparison group selected the same way; check whether change exceeds regression. [ch02]
**Cost**: sample, ethics approvals.
**Not when**: the effect is huge and instantaneous.

## 3. A harsh manager is "proved right"
**When**: criticism is followed by improvement, praise by decline.
**Move**: recognise regression; track distributions; test feedback styles. [ch02]
**Cost**: patience.
**Not when**: a randomised comparison shows a real difference.

## 4. A positive result on a rare condition
**When**: a screening or alert fires on a low-prevalence event.
**Move**: compute natural frequencies; confirm with an independent second test. [ch03]
**Cost**: extra test and delay.
**Not when**: the person is high-risk (prior is higher).

## 5. Refusing every favourable small bet
**When**: loss aversion blocks good decisions.
**Move**: treat each as one of many; set a policy; check ruin risk first. [ch03]
**Cost**: variance in the short run.
**Not when**: a single loss is ruinous or bets are correlated.

## 6. Nothing can change my mind
**When**: someone asserts certainty or says it is hopeless.
**Move**: name the prior as 0 or 1; do not argue evidence; instead create an experiment that can generate new data. [ch03]
**Cost**: little.
**Not when**: logical necessity.

## 7. The repeated falsehood
**When**: a claim keeps being repeated and starts to "feel known".
**Move**: look up the original source; slow down; ask what you would believe if you had heard it once. [ch04]
**Cost**: effort.
**Not when**: familiar everyday knowledge.

## 8. A story outperforms the data
**When**: an anecdote with a villain beats a consensus.
**Move**: translate the aggregate into a case or a short story; give the base rate with the anecdote. (position) [ch04]
**Cost**: communication craft.
**Not when**: the audience is doing the analysis itself.

## 9. Smart people on both sides, more polarised
**When**: identity-laden data question.
**Move**: use neutral framing, concrete policies, curiosity prompts; do not shame. [ch04]
**Cost**: slower campaign.
**Not when**: non-identity topics.

## 10. Charismatic certainty
**When**: a confident person is promoted or believed.
**Move**: ask for probabilities and a record; compare with an outside view. [ch05]
**Cost**: social friction.
**Not when**: scored forecasting culture.

## 11. Is this expert a real expert?
**When**: choosing whose judgment to trust.
**Move**: check the four conditions; prefer a simple formula in noisy settings. [ch05]
**Cost**: building the formula and data.
**Not when**: no stable data.

## 12. Stellar single study
**When**: surprising result, one group, p just under 0.05.
**Move**: wait for replication; check preregistration, number of outcomes, sample size. [ch06]
**Cost**: delay.
**Not when**: strong prior and large pre-registered trial.

## 13. Press release and headline
**When**: big claim about a breakthrough.
**Move**: read the data in the paper; compare the claim with the previous record; look for independent confirmation. [ch06]
**Cost**: time.
**Not when**: reproduced widely.

## 14. Expert evidence in court
**When**: a pattern-matching technique is offered as certain.
**Move**: ask for validation studies and error rates; use blind verification; hide irrelevant case context. [ch06]
**Cost**: slower lab work.
**Not when**: technique validated with known ground truth.

## 15. Forecast far into a chaotic future
**When**: weather-like or market-like systems.
**Move**: use ensembles and ranges; compare with long-run averages; predict statistics, not paths. [ch07]
**Cost**: less crisp messages.
**Not when**: stable system.

## 16. "I can beat the market"
**When**: active picking claims.
**Move**: compare with a cheap index over a decade; assume most outperformance is luck; check that a method survives out-of-sample. [ch05, ch07]
**Cost**: forgoing the story.
**Not when**: a measured repeatable edge.

## 17. Arms race, price war, commons
**When**: a one-shot dilemma.
**Move**: change the game: repetition, uncertain end, verification, small steps. [ch08]
**Cost**: building institutions.
**Not when**: payoffs already favour cooperation.

## 18. Choosing how to vote or decide
**When**: three or more options.
**Move**: list the fairness properties wanted; consider approval or scores; accept the trade. [ch08]
**Cost**: explaining a new method.
**Not when**: two options.

## 19. Credible commitment
**When**: threats are not believable because carrying them out is irrational.
**Move**: bind yourself in advance (automatic rule, visible constraint) and make it visible. [ch08]
**Cost**: risk if error or misjudgment.
**Not when**: errors in the trigger are unacceptable.

## 20. "It's impossible" belief
**When**: a field treats a goal as impossible by consensus.
**Move**: ask whether it is a theorem or a method limit; try a different method or invite an outsider. (position) [ch09, ch10]
**Cost**: failed attempts.
**Not when**: proven impossibility.

## 21. Matching teaching to a "style"
**When**: a team plans around learning styles.
**Move**: drop matching; use multiple formats, active questions, chunking. [ch11]
**Cost**: replacing a popular belief.
**Not when**: task requires one modality.

## 22. A single IQ-like number to rank people
**When**: using a test score for decisions.
**Move**: state effect size, caveats (motivation, coaching, culture); never use as worth. (position) [ch11]
**Cost**: less simplicity.
**Not when**: standardised score used to find overlooked talent, alongside other evidence.

## 23. Inventor unrewarded or invention misused
**When**: designing incentives or releasing a dual-use technology.
**Move**: define sharing and ownership up front; consider misuse and access controls. [ch10]
**Cost**: legal and design effort.
**Not when**: no commercial or safety stakes.
