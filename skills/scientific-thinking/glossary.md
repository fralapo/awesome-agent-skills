# Glossary

**80/20 rule (Pareto principle)** - rule of thumb that about 20% of causes yield 80% of effects (ch15)
**Active learning** - formats that force learners to answer, solve and discuss rather than only listen (ch11)
**Aphantasia** - lack of voluntary mental imagery; part of a spectrum (ch13)
**Apparent weight** - the force a support must exert; zero in free fall (ch17)
**Approval voting** - ballot where voters tick all acceptable candidates (ch08)
**Arrow's impossibility theorem** - no ranked-ballot rule meets five reasonable conditions with three or more options (ch08)
**Attractor** - region of state space toward which a system's trajectories move (ch07)
**Base rate / prior** - how common something is before new evidence (ch03)
**Bayes' theorem** - rule for updating a probability given evidence (ch03)
**Bifurcation (period doubling)** - change in long-run behaviour, e.g. from one value to two, four, eight (ch07)
**Bounded gap** - a fixed distance within which pairs of primes recur infinitely often (ch09)
**Brazil nut effect** - gentle shaking brings large pieces to the top of a mixture (ch17)
**Busy beaver** - longest-running halting program of a given size; connects to the halting problem (ch09)
**Calibration** - match between stated confidence and actual accuracy (ch05)
**Cardinal / ordinal number** - size of a collection / position in an order (ch14)
**Causal vs evidential decision theory** - choose by what you cause vs by what your choice indicates (ch08)
**Chaos** - deterministic behaviour with sensitive dependence on initial conditions (ch07)
**Chunking** - grouping information into larger units using prior knowledge (ch04, ch05)
**Cognitive ease** - feeling of fluency that makes statements seem true (ch04)
**Computer-assisted proof** - proof that depends on exhaustive machine checking (ch09)
**Condorcet cycle (paradox)** - majority preferences that loop: A over B over C over A (ch08)
**Confirmation bias** - preferential search for agreeing evidence (ch04)
**Contextual bias** - influence of case information on expert judgment (ch06)
**Controlled comparison** - control group handled like the treated group (ch02)
**Deliberate practice** - effortful practice at the edge of ability with feedback (ch05)
**Delta hedging (dynamic hedging)** - holding stock to offset an option's risk and rebalancing (ch07)
**Diagonal argument** - construction showing the reals cannot be listed (ch09)
**Dunning-Kruger effect** - low performers overrate, top performers slightly underrate; partly statistical (ch05)
**Efficient market hypothesis** - prices already reflect information; changes are unpredictable (ch07)
**Emergence** - properties of a whole that its parts do not have (ch15)
**Ensemble forecast** - many runs with perturbed inputs to show spread (ch07)
**Fluid vs crystallised intelligence** - new-problem solving vs accumulated knowledge (ch11)
**Flynn effect** - rise of raw IQ scores over decades (ch11)
**Gambler's fallacy** - belief that past outcomes make the opposite outcome due (ch02)
**Great Filter** - hypothetical step that almost no life passes; behind or ahead of us (ch16)
**Halting problem** - no general algorithm decides whether every program stops (ch09)
**Heritability** - share of variation in a population attributed to genes; not individual fixedness (ch11)
**Heuristic (mathematical)** - estimate that matches data but is not a proof (ch09)
**Hydrostatic paradox** - force on a base set by liquid height, not liquid amount (ch17)
**Illusion of truth** - repeated statements feel more true (ch04)
**Inclusion-exclusion** - counting by subtracting and adding back overlaps (ch09)
**Incompleteness** - consistent arithmetic systems have true unprovable statements (ch09)
**Independence of irrelevant alternatives** - group ranking of A vs B should not depend on C (ch08)
**Instant runoff (ranked choice)** - eliminate lowest candidate and transfer ballots (ch08)
**Law of large numbers** - averages converge as trials grow (ch09)
**Learning styles (matching hypothesis)** - unsupported claim that matching teaching to preferred modality improves learning (ch11)
**Logistic map** - simple growth equation showing order and chaos (ch07)
**Loss aversion** - losses weigh about twice as much as equal gains (ch03)
**Mandelbrot set** - parameters of a simple complex map whose iterations stay bounded (ch14)
**Markov chain** - process whose next step depends only on the current state (ch09)
**Median voter** - voter at the middle of a one-dimensional spectrum (ch08)
**Monte Carlo method** - estimating by repeated random sampling (ch09)
**Motivated numeracy** - skilled reasoning used to protect identity-laden conclusions (ch04)
**Multimedia effect** - words with matching pictures beat either alone (ch11)
**Multiple comparisons** - many tests inflate the chance of a false positive (ch02)
**Newcomb's problem** - choice game against a reliable predictor (ch08)
**Null hypothesis** - assumption of no effect (ch02)
**P versus NP** - open question whether quick checking implies quick solving (ch09)
**P-hacking** - analytic choices that push p below threshold (ch06)
**p-value** - probability of data at least this extreme if the null is true (ch02)
**Periodisation** - dividing continuous time into named eras (ch13)
**Phantom entity** - invented substance explaining an effect of apparatus or a known process (ch01)
**Phase space** - plot of all possible states of a system (ch07)
**Power set** - set of all subsets; always a bigger size (ch14)
**Pre-registration (registered report)** - declaring plan and committing to publication regardless of result (ch06)
**Prisoner's dilemma** - game where defection dominates but mutual cooperation is better (ch08)
**Problem of the many** - no sharp boundary, so many candidates for one object (ch13)
**Publication bias** - preferential publication of significant results (ch06)
**Random walk** - path of independent up/down steps (ch07)
**Regression to the mean** - extreme outcomes tend to be followed by less extreme ones (ch02)
**Replication** - independent repetition of a study (ch06)
**Resonance** - timed pushes that add to the energy already present (ch17)
**Sensitive dependence** - tiny input differences amplify (ch07)
**Sorites sequence** - removing small pieces one at a time with no clear point of change (ch13)
**Spoiler effect** - a similar candidate splits votes and changes the winner (ch08)
**Substitution** - answering an easier question than the one asked (ch03)
**Supertask** - infinitely many steps in finite time (ch14)
**Tit for tat** - cooperate first, then copy the partner's last move (ch08)
**Turing completeness** - ability to simulate any computation; brings undecidability (ch09)
**Undecidable** - no algorithm decides all instances in finite time (ch09)
**Universality** - same statistical pattern in unrelated systems (ch15)
**Valid environment** - setting with learnable regularities (ch05)
**Variance explained** - square of the correlation coefficient (ch02)
**Zipf's law** - frequency inversely proportional to rank (ch15)
