# Glossary

**Anchor** — the first credible number on the table; it frames all later concessions (ch04)
**Awareness before sale** — educating a market that does not know it needs the service before selling it (ch10)
**Bid** — time-and-materials price for a defined scope; a gamble when scope is unknown (ch07)
**Blended rate** — average hourly rate across roles used to price a team job (ch03)
**Bonus (instead of discount)** — low-cost extra added to hold the price (ch08, ch11)
**Bracket / price bracketing** — saying a range aloud, higher number first, and reading the reaction (ch04)
**Change order** — separate written estimate for a request outside the agreed scope (ch09)
**Charity vs client** — triage: a clear cause with limited means versus a commercial business seeking free work (ch08)
**Commodity** — an offer that wins only on price; no sales advantage plus premium (ch10)
**Compromise effect** — tendency to pick the middle option of three (ch04)
**Conditional close** — "if we deliver X, would you start?" making a decision feel small (ch06)
**Conditional question** — "if this matters, you should probably not hire us," making the client test a criterion (ch08)
**Contingency** — openly stated reserve over the bid (about 15% for undefined scope) (ch03)
**Cost-plus bid** — people, overhead, contingency, then profit (ch03)
**Day rate** — price per booked day; never less than one day (ch02)
**Deal memo** — one-page agreement sent after verbal commitment (ch06)
**Diagnose before prescribe** — questioning the problem before naming a solution or price (ch05)
**Discovery (paid)** — fixed-fee phase that defines scope, budget and approvals before the build (ch05, ch07)
**Embrace and pivot** — accept the objection, let the client defend, redirect to value and difference (ch08)
**ERRC** — eliminate, reduce, raise, create: four actions to design uncontested space (ch10)
**Exposure** — attention offered as payment; worth something only if measurable and contracted (ch08)
**Fisher** — indecisive prospect gathering information, asks price first (ch05)
**Five whys** — asking "why" three to five times to reach the root problem (ch05)
**Floor** — the cost-based number below which the job loses money (ch03)
**Hybrid fixed + variable** — fixed price for defined phases, variable for undefined parts (ch02)
**Input / output / outcome pricing** — time, deliverables, value (ch02)
**Log line** — two-sentence description: who you are, credentials, whom you serve (ch06)
**Magnitude-of-scale question** — "is this a 50k or a 500k project?" (ch04)
**Markup (for underestimation)** — extra on labour rates because specialists underestimate (ch03)
**MLE (minimum level of engagement)** — annual target ÷ clients served at once = minimum new-client fee (ch05)
**Option ladder** — three priced tiers: premium, core, entry (ch04)
**Passionate detachment** — caring about the client's outcome while neutral about winning (ch06)
**Pitch** — competitive, often unpaid creative work among vendors (ch07)
**Prioritisation grid** — needs scored on important and urgent with a budget per item (ch07)
**Proposal** — self-contained document confirming an agreed conversation (ch07)
**Red flag** — early sign a client will cost more than they pay (ch12)
**Retreat and follow** — stepping back so interested prospects pull toward you (ch06)
**ROM (order-of-magnitude) estimate** — broad range given before scope is known (ch03)
**Rule of three** — three yeses in a row means raise the price (ch12)
**Scope creep** — accumulation of unpriced changes (ch09)
**Square Pegger** — contact who is not the decider and asks literal questions (ch05)
**Statement of work** — project document with scope, assumptions, obligations; used with a master agreement (ch07)
**Tension map** — price, risk, effort/sacrifice and time tensions in an offer (ch11)
**Three-variable test** — budget, timeline, scope: meet only two = weak fit (ch05)
**Time to result / certainty of outcome** — the two drivers of price in an offer (ch10)
**Value-based pricing** — fee as a fraction of the client's measurable gain (ch01)
**Value-equivalence exercise** — pricing non-cash offers in money before accepting a trade (ch08)
**Win-both test** — sales advantage and price premium at the same time (ch10)
