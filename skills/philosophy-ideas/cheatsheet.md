# Philosophy Ideas: Cheatsheet

"Position" = argued stance, not consensus. Chapter references in brackets. Figures are dated and illustrative.

## Where to Start
| Situation | First move |
|---|---|
| Someone gives an argument | Thesis in one sentence, list premises, find the hidden one [ch09] |
| A dilemma is posed | Look for the third option [ch01] |
| "Obviously the chain must stop" | Ask why a stopping point is required [ch01, ch03] |
| A fact is used to justify a duty | State the bridge between is and ought [ch01, ch05] |
| "Everyone has their own truth" | Self-application test [ch01] |
| Whom to believe | Context standard, track record, incentives, check with others [ch02] |
| Viral false claim | Trace to the original; misinformation or disinformation? [ch02] |
| Free will debate | Define "free"; determined or not; randomness is not control [ch03] |
| Consciousness debate | Phenomenal vs access; what does the theory do with experience? [ch04] |
| Moral dilemma | Which theory: outcomes, duty, virtue, Stoic judgment? [ch05] |
| God and morality | Divine-command dilemma; three links, one proves God [ch05] |
| Why obey the law | Comply or obey; four features; consent problem [ch06] |
| Fine-tuning argument | Specific vs general evidence; inverse gambler [ch08] |

## Ethics in One Table
| Theory | Asks | Typical strength | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Utilitarian | What gives the best outcome? | Impartial, practical | Better for whom? may sacrifice individuals [ch05.1] |
| Deontological (Kantian) | What rule could any reasoner will? | Autonomy, responsibility | Formal, rigid [ch05.3] |
| Virtue | What kind of person lives well? | Whole life | Depends on a view of human nature [ch05.5] |
| Stoic | What is up to me? | Practical, emotional | Can read as passivity [ch05.7] |

## Mind in One Table
| Position | Says | Main cost |
|---|---|---|
| Dualism | Mind and body are separate | Interaction; the self-awareness case assumes what it proves [ch04.1] |
| Physicalism | Only the physical exists | Experience left over? [ch04.2] |
| Representational / vague | Consciousness is a model; no sharp line | The remainder is dissolved, not answered [ch04.3-4.4] |
| Eliminativism | No qualia, no intentionality | Meaning-using claim [ch04.5] |
| Panpsychism | Proto-consciousness in matter | Defers; summing problem [ch04.5] |
| Functionalism | Function is what counts | AI mind depends on the theory [ch04.8] |

## Free Will in One Table
| View | Claim | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Hard determinism | No alternatives; no desert | Blame loses footing [ch03.6] |
| Libertarian | I could have done otherwise | Randomness or undetermined self [ch03.5] |
| Compatibilist | Free if uncoerced | Redefines "could" [ch03.5] |
| Capacity view (position) | Free will is envisioning futures | Does it escape the dilemma? [ch03.7] |
