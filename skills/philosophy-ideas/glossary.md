# Glossary

**A-theory / B-theory** - tensed view of time (present privileged) versus tenseless view (earlier/later only) (ch03)
**Acceptance principle** - one may accept what is presented as true and intelligible unless there are stronger reasons not to (ch02)
**Agent-relative value** - a value defined from a particular person's point of view (ch05)
**Anthropic selection** - we can only observe conditions that allow observers (ch08)
**Anti-reductionism (testimony)** - testimony is a basic source of justification (ch02)
**Autonomy** - self-legislation by one's own reason (ch05)
**Bridge premise** - the step that connects a fact to a value (ch01)
**Cardinal virtues (Stoic)** - practical wisdom, temperance, justice, courage (ch05)
**Compatibilism** - freedom is consistent with determinism if there is no coercion (ch03)
**Computational functionalism** - consciousness is a computation, independent of substrate (ch04)
**Content-independent reason** - a reason that does not depend on what the law says (ch06)
**Dialectic** - constant change in which each stage revises the last (ch06)
**Dichotomy of control** - only judgments are fully up to us (ch05)
**Disinformation** - false information intended to mislead (ch02)
**Divine-command dilemma** - is it good because commanded, or commanded because good? (ch05)
**Eliminativism** - denies qualitative states and intrinsic intentionality (ch04)
**Emergence** - explanation of wholes with rules beyond the parts (ch08)
**Epistemic injustice** - a wrong done to someone as a knower (ch02)
**Evidential argument from evil** - given the evils, God is improbable (ch05)
**Flying man** - suspended person without sensation who still knows he exists (ch04)
**Free-will defence** - a world with free agents is better despite evil (ch05)
**Genetic fallacy** - judging a claim by its origin (ch01)
**Greater-good defence** - evils needed for outweighing goods (ch05)
**Hypergraph** - nodes linked by edges that may join more than two nodes (ch03)
**Illicit influence campaign** - covert coordination using assets, money and persons to shape behaviour (ch02)
**Inverse gambler's fallacy** - inferring many trials from a lucky outcome (ch08)
**Is-ought gap** - descriptions do not entail prescriptions (ch01)
**Learned helplessness (information)** - giving up judging amid contradictory material (ch02)
**Logical argument from evil** - evil contradicts an all-good all-powerful being (ch05)
**Logos** - the rational structure of the universe in Stoicism (ch05)
**Marginal utility** - extra satisfaction from one more unit; diminishes (ch05)
**Misinformation** - unintentionally false or misleading information (ch02)
**Naturalism** - nothing beyond what science uncovers; philosophy uses scientific methods (ch04)
**Ought implies can** - a duty requires ability (ch03)
**Phenomenal consciousness** - raw experience, what it is like (ch04)
**Access consciousness** - content available to reasoning and report (ch04)
**Phenomenology** - the study of the structure of experience (ch07)
**Physicalism** - reality is exhausted by the physical (ch04)
**Political obligation** - a special reason to obey the law because it is the law (ch06)
**Pro tanto** - applies unless overridden (ch06)
**Rationalism / empiricism** - knowledge by reason versus by experience (ch02)
**Reductionism** - explaining wholes by parts (ch08)
**Reductionism (testimony)** - testimony needs independent evidence of reliability (ch02)
**Regress** - chain of "and what caused that?" (ch01, ch03)
**Relativism** - truth or value depends on the judge (ch01)
**Requirement of total evidence** - use the most specific evidence available (ch08)
**Selective exposure** - sorting into communities that confirm one's views (ch02)
**Sortition** - selection of officials by lot (ch06)
**Supervenience** - no difference in the higher facts without a difference in the physical (ch04)
**Teleology** - explanation by purpose or value (ch04, ch08)
**Testimonial injustice** - prejudice blocks a person from being believed (ch02)
**Thing in itself** - reality as it is apart from our experience (ch02, ch07)
**True-world theory** - doctrine that a truer reality lies behind this one (ch07)
**Unmoved mover** - an unmoved first cause of change (ch03)
**Utilitarianism** - the right act produces the best outcome (ch05)
**Vulnerability problem** - trusting testimony exposes you to error or lies (ch02)
