# Glossary

Italian term -> English meaning, with chapter. Educational, not advice. Rules are snapshots; verify.

**730 (modello 730)** - simplified personal tax return for employees and pensioners; does not cover all foreign-broker sections (ch06)
**Ammortamento alla francese** - constant-payment amortisation: early payments mostly interest (ch08)
**Ammortamento all'italiana** - fixed-principal amortisation: falling payment (ch08)
**Appropriatezza (test di)** - appropriateness test for execution-only trading: knowledge and experience (ch11)
**Bail-in** - loss absorption by bank creditors in resolution (ch03)
**BOT** - short discount government bill (ch02)
**Bollo** - stamp duty: on securities 0.2% of value, on current accounts above a balance threshold (ch01, ch02)
**BTP** - fixed-coupon government bond (ch02)
**Inflation-linked BTP / retail inflation-linked issue** - government bonds with coupon or principal linked to inflation (ch02)
**Retail step-up issue** - retail government bond with step-up coupons and a loyalty bonus, sometimes with an early-redemption option (ch02)
**Buoni fruttiferi postali** - state-guaranteed postal savings bonds (ch01)
**CCT** - floating-rate government bond (ch02)
**Cedolare secca** - flat tax on rental income (ch08)
**Conto cointestato** - joint account; indivisible; can be frozen (ch01, ch09)
**CTZ** - zero-coupon government bond (ch02)
**Dichiarativo (regime)** - declarative regime: investor computes and reports (ch04, ch06)
**F24** - unified payment form for taxes (ch06)
**Fondo pensione** - supplementary pension fund (ch07)
**Forfettario (regime)** - flat-rate regime for small self-employed (ch10)
**Gestito (risparmio)** - managed regime: tax on year-end result (ch04)
**Amministrato (risparmio)** - administered regime: intermediary withholds (ch04)
**IMU** - municipal property tax (ch08)
**INPS** - national social security institute: public pension (ch07)
**IRPEF** - progressive personal income tax (ch07, ch10)
**ISIN** - securities identifier; a retail issue's bonus and put are tied to the placement ISIN (ch02)
**IVAFE** - levy on foreign financial assets (ch06)
**Legittima (quota)** - reserved share of an estate (ch09)
**Minusvalenza** - capital loss (ch05)
**Zainetto fiscale** - informal name for the stock of carried-forward losses (ch05)
**Plusvalenza** - capital gain (ch05, ch08)
**Mutuo** - mortgage (ch08)
**Surroga** - transfer of a mortgage to another lender (ch08)
**PIP** - individual insurance-based pension plan (ch07)
**PIR** - domestic personal savings plan with tax exemption under conditions (ch10)
**Prestazione occasionale** - occasional self-employment (ch10)
**Prima casa** - first home; reduced transfer taxes if conditions are met (ch08)
**Quadro RW** - tax-return section monitoring foreign assets (ch06)
**Quadro RT** - section for capital gains and losses (ch06)
**Quadro RM** - section for separately taxed income, e.g. foreign dividends (ch06)
**Ravvedimento operoso** - voluntary correction of a late or wrong filing with reduced penalties (ch06)
**Redditi di capitale** - capital income: dividends, coupons, fund gains; cannot be offset by losses (ch04)
**Redditi diversi** - miscellaneous income: gains on stocks, certificates, etc.; can be offset (ch04)
**Rateo** - accrued interest (ch02)
**RITA** - bridge annuity from a pension fund before public pension age (ch07)
**Rendimento a scadenza** - yield to maturity (ch02)
**Successione** - succession; statutory and reserved shares (ch09)
**TFR** - end-of-service benefit accrued by employees (ch07)
**UCITS (armonizzato)** - harmonised fund; fund gains are capital income (ch04)
**White list** - list of states whose bonds get the 12.5% rate; fund rate uses the share of such bonds (ch04)
