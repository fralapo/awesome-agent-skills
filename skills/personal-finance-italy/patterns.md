# Patterns

Recurring situation -> move. Each entry: When / Move / Cost / Not when. Chapter in brackets. Educational, not advice; figures are snapshots to verify.

## 1. "This bond pays much more"
**When**: a list sorted by yield shows outliers.
**Move**: ask what risk is being paid for: issuer, ranking, call, currency, lock-up, annualisation of a short horizon. [ch03]
**Cost**: fewer exciting candidates.
**Not when**: the pick-up is a small, understood mispricing on a short high-grade bond.

## 2. Comparing across tax regimes
**When**: a government bond is compared to a corporate bond or deposit.
**Move**: convert to net, then to the gross-equivalent government yield (net / 0.875). [ch02, ch03]
**Cost**: extra arithmetic.
**Not when**: the rates have changed; update the snapshot first.

## 3. "I have a loss, so my gain is covered"
**When**: an ETF loss sits next to an ETF gain.
**Move**: check the category: the loss is miscellaneous and cannot shelter capital income. Look for stock or certificate gains. [ch04, ch05]
**Cost**: the loss may go unused.
**Not when**: a reform unifies the categories (verify).

## 4. Step-up or call-plus-step-up
**When**: coupons rise over time and the issuer can call.
**Move**: compute the IRR on dated flows and the called scenario; do not average coupons. [ch03]
**Cost**: modest.
**Not when**: non-callable and held to maturity; IRR is exact.

## 5. Buying between coupons
**When**: dirty price differs from the quote.
**Move**: reconstruct the dirty price (cash / quantity); remember tax credit on accrued; for inflation-linked bonds check the pre-paid indexation. [ch02]
**Cost**: attention.
**Not when**: bought at par on a coupon date.

## 6. Accumulating versus distributing
**When**: choosing between two share classes.
**Move**: assume equal pre-tax return; prefer deferral unless cash flow is the point; note that distribution taxes at 26% every year. [ch04]
**Cost**: selling units when cash is needed.
**Not when**: tiny reinvestments with fixed commissions.

## 7. Foreign broker, first filing
**When**: a foreign account has holdings, perhaps no sales.
**Move**: rebuild lots from the first purchase, apply the right method, compute holding days, fill monitoring first. [ch06]
**Cost**: time or an accountant.
**Not when**: a domestic intermediary withholds.

## 8. Pension fund vs ETF
**When**: choosing where a monthly amount goes.
**Move**: gross-up at the marginal rate, add match and TFR, compare by horizon with costs. [ch07]
**Cost**: lock-up and rules.
**Not when**: no tax to deduct and no match: the case weakens.

## 9. Fixed or variable mortgage
**When**: financing a purchase.
**Move**: price the portability option; check the maximum possible payment, not the average. [ch08]
**Cost**: a slightly higher initial rate.
**Not when**: the right to transfer is removed or spreads are very wide.

## 10. Rent versus buy
**When**: someone asks which is better.
**Move**: build both capital paths with all owner costs and the alternative return; treat appreciation as uncertain. [ch08]
**Cost**: assumptions dominate.
**Not when**: the renter cannot invest the difference productively.

## 11. Rental flat net yield
**When**: gross yield looks attractive.
**Move**: subtract tax, vacancies, charges, maintenance, renovation reserve; compare with the break-even sale price. [ch08]
**Cost**: modest.
**Not when**: appreciation, expertise or a deep discount change the case.

## 12. Heirs and a big gain
**When**: an old holder has large unrealised gains.
**Move**: check the category: death triggers no income tax; funds and ETFs pass with the original basis (tax on the whole gain at the heir's sale), miscellaneous-income instruments generally take the declared succession value (verify). [ch09]
**Cost**: none; just a decision.
**Not when**: rules change or the spouse exemptions apply.

## 13. Wrapper with a promised exemption
**When**: a product sells a tax saving.
**Move**: compute tax avoided minus fee and constraints; test the fragility of compliance. [ch10]
**Cost**: modest.
**Not when**: capital is large and returns exceed the fee.

## 14. Guaranteed, fast, urgent
**When**: an offer arrives unsolicited.
**Move**: stop; verify the registry and domain; call back on a number you found; refuse prepaid fees. [ch11]
**Cost**: a missed real opportunity occasionally.
**Not when**: you initiated contact with an authorised intermediary.

## 15. "A forum said..."
**When**: a rule is quoted without a year.
**Move**: climb the source ladder to primary law or official guidance for the year; record source and date. [ch12]
**Cost**: time.
**Not when**: the statement is a definition, not date-bound.
