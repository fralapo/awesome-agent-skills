# Chapter 4: Tooling and Measurement of AI Visibility

## Core Idea
In 2025-2026 a category of tools emerged that is dedicated to measuring the "AI visibility" of brands — presence, share of voice and sentiment in the answers of ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Google AI Overviews. It is analogous to the rank trackers of classic SEO but built from scratch for an output that is no longer a list of links. The category is crowded, its funding is growing fast, and it still lacks an established measurement standard.

## What the tools measure in general

- **Common methodology**: the tools run repeated prompts/queries against the main AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Google AI Overviews, sometimes Claude) and track over time: presence/frequency of citation of the brand, share of voice against competitors on a set of queries/topics, sentiment of the mention (positive/neutral/negative), and sometimes the position of the citation in the answer, the sources cited together with the brand, and the distinction between linked and unlinked mentions.

## Profound

- **Funding**: the best-funded player in the category — **$96M Series C at a $1B valuation** (Feb 2026), total funding **$155M**.
- **Positioning**: enterprise/agencies.

## Peec AI

- **Profile**: a European startup, launched in mid-2025.
- **Growth**: fast — **€650K ARR in 4 months**, then an **€18M Series A** round (Nov 2025).
- **Positioning**: a good depth-to-price ratio between Otterly and Profound, strong on share-of-voice analytics and competitive benchmarking.

## Otterly.AI

- **Price**: the lowest entry point in the category — plans starting from **$29/month**.
- **Target**: suited to small teams/SMBs that want to start monitoring without an enterprise budget.

## AthenaHQ

- **Distinctive feature**: it can show a prospect's real AI visibility during a sales call — widely used by agencies for selling and demonstration.

## Other tools mentioned in the landscape

- **Goodie**, the **Semrush AI toolkit** (an add-on inside Semrush) and **Ahrefs Brand Radar** (an add-on inside Ahrefs) are cited as examples of established SEO suites integrating AI-visibility modules instead of leaving the space only to dedicated startups.
- **Market consolidation**: comparative industry sources also publish lists such as "18 best AI visibility tools" / "12 best AI search tools" — the category is crowded and consolidating quickly, with large funding rounds in 2025-2026 that point to foreseeable M&A.

## Important methodological note

- **Estimates, not a census**: these tools sample a set of prompts/queries — the results are estimates, not a real census of AI traffic, which the AI platforms do not expose publicly at a granular level the way Google Search Console does for classic search.
- **Low overlap between platforms**: only **11%** of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity (see `ch02-how-generative-engines-work.md`) — this low overlap means multi-platform tracking is necessary: a single engine does not represent the whole "AI search landscape".

## How to choose

A practical heuristic drawn from the comparative guides:

- **Low budget / starting to monitor**: Otterly.AI.
- **In-depth competitive benchmarking / share of voice**: Peec AI.
- **Enterprise / large agencies with reporting needs**: Profound.
- **Sales enablement / demonstrating value to a prospect**: AthenaHQ.
- **Already a Semrush/Ahrefs customer**: the comparative guides suggest evaluating the integrated AI toolkit/Brand Radar modules first, before adding a third-party tool.

## Key Takeaways
1. The AI-visibility tool market measures brand presence, share of voice and sentiment on repeated prompts against the main AI engines — it is the conceptual equivalent of the SEO rank tracker, but without an established measurement standard.
2. Profound is the best-funded player ($96M Series C, $1B valuation in Feb 2026, $155M total funding) and is positioned for enterprise; Peec AI is a fast-growing European startup (€650K ARR in 4 months, then an €18M Series A in Nov 2025) that is strong on share-of-voice analytics.
3. Otterly.AI has the lowest entry point in the category (plans from $29/month), while AthenaHQ stands out for demonstrating a prospect's real AI visibility during sales calls.
4. The established SEO suites (Semrush, Ahrefs) are integrating AI-visibility modules as add-ons (AI toolkit, Brand Radar) instead of leaving the category only to dedicated startups.
5. The data from these tools are sampling-based estimates, not a real census — and since only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity, multi-platform tracking is needed, not a single engine.
