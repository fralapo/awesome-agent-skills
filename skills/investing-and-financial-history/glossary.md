# Glossary

**Accrued interest** - coupon earned since the last payment, paid by the buyer to the seller (ch06)
**Alpha** - return not explained by a pricing model's factors (ch03)
**Arithmetic of active management** - before costs the average active dollar equals the average passive dollar; after costs it is lower (ch02)
**Asset allocation** - split of a portfolio between asset classes, set by risk profile (ch04)
**Autocallable** - certificate that redeems early above a trigger and repays by the worst performer below a barrier (ch07)
**Backtest** - replay of a rule on historical data; a story from one sample (ch13)
**Behavioural loss tolerance** - ability to hold through a fall without selling (ch01)
**Beta** - sensitivity of an asset to the market (ch01, ch03)
**Bond** - tradable loan with coupons and principal at maturity (ch06)
**Book-to-market** - accounting value relative to market value; high = value stock (ch03)
**Bounded rationality** - settling for a satisfactory answer instead of the optimal one (ch08)
**Break-even inflation** - inflation at which an inflation-linked bond matches a fixed one (ch06)
**CAPE** - price over 10-year average real earnings (ch12)
**Catastrophic loss** - a fall of 70% from peak never recovered (ch03)
**Clean price** - quoted price excluding accrued interest (ch06)
**Confirmation bias** - weighing supportive evidence more (ch08)
**Convexity** - curvature of the price-yield relation; second-order correction to duration (ch06)
**Correlation** - how much two assets move together (ch04)
**Cost basis** - purchase cost used to compute gain; averages on purchase (ch12)
**Cost of capital** - return required for the risk of a business; the discount rate (ch05)
**Covered call** - owning a stock and selling a call on it (ch07)
**Credit default swap** - derivative paying on a borrower's default (ch07, ch09)
**Daily-reset leverage** - product multiplying each day's return by L (ch07)
**DCF** - valuation by discounting forecast free cash flows (ch05)
**Decumulation** - drawing down savings in retirement (ch14)
**Delta / gamma / theta / vega / rho** - option Greeks (ch07)
**Disposition effect** - selling winners early, holding losers (ch08)
**Diversification** - spreading holdings so outcomes are not identical (ch04)
**Drawdown** - fall from the running peak (ch12)
**Duration** - weighted-average time of payments; approximate rate sensitivity (ch06)
**Efficient markets** - prices fully reflect available information (ch02)
**Endowment effect** - valuing what you own above what you would pay (ch08)
**Ex-date** - first day a buyer no longer receives the dividend; price falls by about it (ch04, ch12)
**Factor zoo** - the proliferation of published factors (ch03)
**Factor** - a characteristic that explains return differences (size, value, profitability, investment, momentum) (ch03)
**Fair game** - the model in which price changes are unpredictable because expectations adapt to news (ch02)
**Forward multiple** - price over expected earnings (ch05)
**Geometric mean** - compounded average return per period (ch12)
**Idiosyncratic risk** - company-specific, diversifiable, uncompensated risk (ch01, ch04)
**Illusion of control** - overestimating influence on outcomes (ch08)
**Inverted yield curve** - short yields above long yields (ch06)
**IRR** - rate that sets NPV to zero; assumes reinvestment at itself (ch12)
**Joint hypothesis problem** - efficiency can only be tested with a model of normal returns (ch02)
**Limits to arbitrage** - reasons a mispricing may persist (ch02, ch03)
**Liquidity premium** - extra yield for tying money up longer (ch06)
**Loss aversion** - a loss hurts more than an equal gain pleases (ch08)
**Lump sum vs staged entry** - investing at once versus in tranches (ch04)
**Margin call** - demand for more collateral on a leveraged position (ch07)
**Mental accounting** - treating money differently by label (ch08)
**Modified IRR** - IRR with an explicit reinvestment rate (ch12)
**Multiple** - ratio of price to a fundamental (ch05)
**NPV** - sum of flows discounted at a personal rate (ch12)
**Out of the money** - an option not currently profitable to exercise (ch07)
**Overlapping windows** - rolling periods sharing data; not independent samples (ch13)
**Persistence** - whether past winners repeat (ch02)
**Ponzi scheme** - pays earlier investors with later investors' money (ch10)
**Portfolio insurance** - synthetic option strategy that sells as prices fall (ch07, ch09)
**Positive skew** - most stocks lag, a few mega-winners dominate (ch03)
**Pyramid scheme** - structure paid by recruiting; fails by arithmetic (ch10)
**Quantitative easing** - central-bank purchases of assets with new reserves (ch06)
**Random walk** - price changes without a predictable pattern (ch02)
**Rebalancing** - restoring target weights; controls risk, not return (ch04)
**Reflexivity** - prices shape the fundamentals they reflect (ch09)
**Risk composure** - actual behaviour in a decline (ch01)
**Rogue trader** - employee trading beyond authority and hiding losses (ch10)
**Seniority** - order of repayment in bankruptcy (ch06)
**Sequence-of-returns risk** - damage from bad early returns while withdrawing (ch14)
**Sign test** - counts wins against a binomial with p = 0.5 (ch13)
**Step-up coupon** - coupon rising on a schedule; IRR below the mean coupon (ch06)
**Survivorship bias** - testing only on firms that survived (ch13)
**Target-maturity fund** - bond fund holding one maturity window, dissolved then (ch06)
**Terminal value** - value of cash flows beyond the forecast period (ch05)
**Tracking difference** - fund return minus index return (ch04)
**Trailing multiple** - price over past earnings (ch05)
**Value at risk (VaR)** - percentile of returns marking the start of the tail (ch12)
**Value premium** - extra return of cheap versus expensive stocks (ch03)
**Volatility drag** - loss from compounding oscillation: `1 - x^2` per up-down pair (ch07, ch12)
**Volatility** - dispersion of returns, a common proxy for risk (ch01)
**Withdrawal rate** - initial annual spending over capital (ch14)
**Yield curve** - yields plotted against maturity (ch06)
**Yield to maturity** - single rate discounting all flows to the price paid (ch06)
**Yield** - income as a percentage of price (ch06)
**Circular financing** - an investor funds a customer that uses the money to buy from the investor (ch09, Card I)
**Remaining performance obligations (RPO)** - signed but not yet delivered or recognised contract revenue (ch09, Card I)
**Useful-life assumption** - years over which an asset is depreciated; a longer life raises reported profit (ch05.6)
**Buyback (share repurchase)** - firm cancels shares, raising earnings per share on the same profit (ch05.6)
