# Glossary

Educational, not medical advice. Terms are used as concepts; chapter in brackets.

**Allodynia** - pain from a stimulus that is normally not painful (light pressure, stroking, mild cold or warmth) (ch02)
**Anterolateral ligament** - knee ligament adding control of internal tibial rotation (ch06)
**Avascular necrosis** - bone death after loss of blood supply, as at the femoral head (ch06)
**Base rate (prevalence)** - how common a condition is in a given group before any test (ch01)
**Biopsychosocial model** - view of a problem across biological, psychological and social levels (ch03)
**Butt wink** - posterior pelvic tilt at the bottom of a squat, often when hip flexion reaches end range (ch07)
**Central sensitisation** - increased responsiveness of central nervous system pain pathways (ch02)
**Clinical vs statistical significance** - whether an effect matters to a person vs whether it is unlikely under a null hypothesis (ch01)
**Dynamics / statics** - motion with acceleration vs without (ch04)
**Envelope of function** - load-by-frequency boundary within which tissue homeostasis holds; individual and changeable (ch05)
**Extrusion / protrusion / sequestration** - stages of disc herniation (ch06)
**Fibroblastic phase** - second healing phase, collagen scaffold, about day 3 to week 3 (ch05)
**Free-body diagram** - drawing of all forces on one object (ch04)
**Gait cycle** - contact to next contact of the same foot; stance about 60 to 62%, swing the rest (ch04)
**Goldilocks principle** - too little, too much, and a moving just-right amount of load (ch05)
**Ground reaction force** - equal and opposite force the ground applies in response to loading (ch04)
**Kinematics / kinetics** - description of motion vs the forces and torques that cause it (ch04)
**Labrum** - fibrocartilage rim that deepens the shoulder socket (ch06)
**Likelihood ratio** - how much a test result changes the odds of a condition (ch01)
**Loaded movement** - gradually loading an injured area as part of healing (ch05)
**Moment arm** - perpendicular distance from the line of force to the axis of rotation (ch04)
**Motion segment** - two adjacent vertebrae with the disc and nerve roots between them (ch04)
**Neutral zone** - range around a joint's resting position with little resistance; variable in the spine (ch07)
**Nocebo** - harm from negative expectation or threatening information (ch02)
**Nociception** - detection of potentially threatening stimuli by receptors; input, not pain (ch02)
**Nociplastic pain** - pain from altered processing without explaining damage or nerve lesion (ch02)
**Plantar fascia** - thick band from heel to toes that tensions in propulsion (ch06)
**Pre-test probability** - estimate of the chance of a condition before a test (ch01)
**Primary / secondary pain (position)** - pain as the problem itself vs pain driven by an ongoing source (ch02)
**Red flag** - sign or pattern suggesting serious pathology that needs medical review (ch03)
**Remodelling** - late healing phase where tissue reorganises, weeks to years (ch05)
**Repetitions in reserve** - number of repetitions left before failure, used to gauge intensity (ch05)
**Sensitivity / specificity** - share of true cases a test detects / share of non-cases it clears (ch01)
**SnNout / SpPin** - mnemonic: sensitive test negative rules out; specific test positive rules in (ch01)
**Special test** - physical examination manoeuvre meant to provoke or confirm a structure's involvement; most have weak standalone value (ch01)
**Specificity (training)** - the adaptation matches the stress applied (ch05)
**Supra-physiological overload** - zone above the envelope where homeostasis is disrupted without immediate failure (ch05)
**Tendinopathy** - umbrella term for persistent load-related tendon pain and reduced function (ch05)
**Torque** - rotational effect of a force: force x perpendicular distance (ch04)
**Warm-up phenomenon** - tendon pain easing once active (ch05)
**Windlass mechanism** - toe extension winds the plantar fascia and lifts the arch (ch06)
**Yellow flag** - psychosocial risk factor for poorer recovery (ch03)
