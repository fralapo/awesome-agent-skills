# Biomechanics and Rehab Reasoning: Cheatsheet

Educational, not medical advice. Figures are dated rules of thumb or single-study snapshots; re-check. "Position" = practitioner judgment, not settled evidence. Chapter references in brackets.

## Where to Start
| Situation | First move |
|---|---|
| "This test proves it" | Ask base rate and LR; a test only helps if it can cross a decision threshold [ch01] |
| Headline study | Read methods, not abstract; effect size, comparison, bias [ch01] |
| Pain without clear damage | Nociception is not pain; context, sleep, stress, beliefs [ch02] |
| Spread pain, light touch hurts | Mechanism category "nociplastic" is a hypothesis; screening is not a diagnosis [ch02] |
| Fear and avoidance | Yellow flags; words are exposure [ch02, ch03] |
| Worrying pattern (night pain, weight loss, fever, trauma, neurological change) | Red-flag pattern: refer to a clinician [ch03] |
| Torque questions | Force x perpendicular distance [ch04] |
| "Too much too soon" | Load vs capacity; graded exposure [ch05] |
| Pain eased, rehab stopped | Remodelling continues for weeks to years (position) [ch05] |
| Shoulder instability | Ball-and-saucer; anterior restraints [ch06] |
| Squat depth | Goal-dependent; specificity; stop at pain or breakdown (position) [ch07] |
| Rounded back lifting | Stiff curve vs motion at one segment; adaptation counter-argument [ch07] |
| Scan result frightening | Findings are common in people without pain [ch08] |
| "Fix your posture" | Weak evidence for most static features [ch09] |
| Claim online | Use the 12-question checklist [ch10] |

## Likelihood Ratios in One Line
LR+ = sens / (1 - spec); LR- = (1 - sens) / spec. Probability -> odds (p/(1-p)) -> multiply by LR -> back to probability (odds/(odds+1)). LR+ above about 10 or LR- below about 0.1 are strong; near 1 is nearly useless. Sn-N-out: a sensitive test, when negative, rules out. Sp-P-in: a specific test, when positive, rules in. [ch01]

## Pain Mechanism Map
| Type | Idea | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Nociceptive | Tissue-related, clear mechanical triggers | Not proof of damage [ch02] |
| Neuropathic | Lesion or disease of the nervous system | Needs assessment |
| Nociplastic | Altered processing; spread, hypersensitivity | Not for local-only pain [ch02] |

## Load and Healing (approximate)
| Idea | Summary |
|---|---|
| Goldilocks | Too little, too much, just right (moves) [ch05] |
| Envelope of function | Load vs frequency; shrinks after overload [ch05] |
| Healing | Inflammatory ~1-3 days; fibroblastic to ~3 weeks; remodelling 4-6 weeks to years [ch05] |
| Tendon | Not simple inflammation; loading rate matters; next-day response informs [ch05] |
| Stretching | Held stretches before power tasks may lower output [ch05] |

## Regional Quick Map
| Region | Mechanism to remember |
|---|---|
| Shoulder | ~1/3 of head in contact; cuff centres the head; more anterior ligaments [ch06] |
| Spine | Muscles as a unit; ligaments by level; "slipped disc" is a misnomer [ch06] |
| Hip | Flexors include the deep lumbar muscle; ~90% blood supply via circumflex ring [ch06] |
| Knee | MCL valgus, LCL varus, ACL forward tibial translation, PCL backward [ch06] |
| Foot | Windlass: toe extension tightens fascia, raises arch [ch06] |
| Hand | Superficial flexor to middle phalanx; deep to distal [ch06] |
| Calf | Two-joint muscle, fast fibres, medial head more injured [ch06] |

## Contested Positions
| Topic | One side | Other side |
|---|---|---|
| Persistent back pain | Capacity, fear and inactivity loop; chronic = duration, not irreversible [ch03] | Overstates inactivity; analgesics and surgery have roles [ch03, ch08] |
| Posture and pain | Weak association; tissues adapt [ch09] | Sustained loads and specific tasks can matter [ch09] |
| Biomechanics | Findings secondary to pain [ch09] | Movement is the means to change load [ch09] |
| Imaging | Poor predictor; harmful if routine [ch08] | Needed for specific questions [ch08] |
| Butt wink | Concentrates disc stress under load [ch07] | Often harmless unloaded; adaptation [ch07] |
| Ice and anti-inflammatories | May delay healing [ch05] | Evidence mixed [ch05] |
| Passive modalities | Dependency, weak evidence [ch08] | Context effects are real [ch08] |
| Primary pain model | Explain pain; stop hunting structure [ch02] | Risk of dismissal; load matters [ch02] |
