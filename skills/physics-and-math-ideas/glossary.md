# Glossary

**Action** - quantity (integral over time of kinetic minus potential energy, or of proper time) that is stationary on real paths (ch01)
**AdS/CFT** - worked example of a holographic duality between a bulk in negatively curved space and a boundary conformal field theory (ch04)
**Aleph-null** - size of the countable infinity of natural numbers (ch08)
**Analytic continuation** - extending a function beyond the region where its defining series converges (ch07)
**Arrow of time** - asymmetry between past and future, from rising entropy plus a low-entropy start (ch06)
**Bekenstein bound** - maximum information a region can hold, proportional to its boundary area (ch04)
**Bell inequalities** - limits obeyed by local hidden-variable theories; violated in experiments (ch03)
**Block universe** - picture where all of time exists together (ch02)
**Born rule** - probability equals the square of the amplitude (ch03)
**Boltzmann brain** - lone fluctuation-made observer with false memories; a reductio of fluctuation cosmologies (ch06)
**Boson** - integer-spin particle; any number can share a state (ch07)
**Bose-Einstein condensate** - state where many bosons occupy the lowest state (ch07)
**Casimir effect** - attraction between plates from excluded vacuum modes (ch07)
**Clique** - set of mutually connected nodes; a standard hard problem (ch08)
**Complementarity** - claim that no observer sees both copies of information near a horizon (ch04)
**Conformal field theory** - theory whose laws do not depend on size scale (ch04)
**Constructible universe** - minimal model containing only what the axioms force; used to show consistency of the continuum hypothesis (ch08)
**Continuum hypothesis** - no infinity between the naturals and the reals; independent of ZFC (ch08)
**Countable (enumerable)** - can be paired with the natural numbers (ch08)
**Covariance (shared uncertainty)** - common error or data between probes (ch01, ch05)
**De Broglie wavelength** - Planck constant divided by momentum (ch03)
**Decoherence** - loss of superposition through entanglement with the environment (ch03)
**Degeneracy** - several parameter sets giving the same observation (ch01, ch05)
**Diagonal argument** - construction showing the reals cannot be listed (ch08)
**Duality** - two descriptions of the same physics (ch04)
**Effective theory (IR/UV)** - low-energy description (IR) of a deeper high-energy theory (UV) (ch01)
**Entropy** - hidden information / log of microstate count / unavailable work (ch06)
**Equivalence principle** - free fall is locally indistinguishable from weightlessness (ch02, ch04)
**Extremal black hole** - maximum spin or charge with a horizon (ch04)
**Fermion** - half-integer-spin particle; one per state (ch07)
**Fine-tuning** - value that requires fortuitous cancellation (ch05)
**Firewall** - proposed energetic barrier at the horizon (ch04)
**Forcing** - method to build larger models of set theory; used to show the continuum hypothesis cannot be proved (ch08)
**Gauge symmetry** - local redundancy that forces a force field (ch01, ch07)
**Geodesic** - path of a free-falling object or light ray (ch02)
**Geodesic incompleteness** - paths that end; the sign of a singularity (ch04)
**Halting problem** - deciding if a program halts; undecidable (ch08)
**Hierarchy problem** - why the Higgs mass is not huge (ch05)
**Higgs mechanism** - symmetry breaking that gives the weak bosons mass (ch07)
**Holographic principle** - bulk information encodable on a boundary (ch04)
**Independence (of a statement)** - neither provable nor refutable from the axioms (ch08)
**Kolmogorov complexity** - length of the shortest description; uncomputable (ch08)
**Lagrangian** - kinetic minus potential energy function (ch01)
**Landauer limit** - minimum heat to erase one bit (ch06)
**Lambda-CDM** - standard cosmological model with a constant and cold dark matter (ch05)
**Macrostate / microstate** - coarse versus full description (ch06)
**Monogamy of entanglement** - a maximally entangled pair cannot also be entangled with a third system (ch04)
**Naturalness** - values not requiring arrangement under an explicit prior (ch05)
**No-hair theorem** - black holes are fixed by mass, spin and charge (ch04)
**NP / NP-complete** - quickly checkable problems / the hardest of them (ch08)
**Page time** - when entanglement between hole and radiation peaks (ch04)
**Path integral** - quantum sum over all paths weighted by phase (ch01)
**Planck constant** - sets the scale of quantum effects (ch03)
**Proper time** - time measured by the moving object (ch01, ch02)
**Regularisation** - taming divergent sums with a cutoff or continuation (ch07)
**Renormalisation** - fixing measured values and discarding divergent parts (ch05)
**Spontaneous symmetry breaking** - asymmetric ground state of a symmetric law (ch07)
**Stationary action** - the more accurate name for "least action" (ch01)
**Timescape** - proposal that time-flow differences mimic dark energy (ch05)
**Trapped surface** - closed surface where outward light moves inward (ch04)
**Turing machine** - tape, head, states; model of effective procedure (ch08)
**Ultraviolet catastrophe** - classical prediction of infinite high-frequency radiation (ch03)
**Undecidable** - no algorithm decides every instance (ch08)
**Unitarity** - conservation of quantum information (ch04)
**Universality** - one machine can run any program (ch08)
**Von Neumann entropy** - quantum entropy; zero for pure states (ch06)
**Wave function** - distribution of possibilities of a quantum system (ch03)
**ZFC** - the standard axioms of set theory (ch08)
