# Physics and Math Ideas: Cheatsheet

Figures are dated; re-check. "Position" = judgment, not fact. Chapter references in brackets.

## Where to Start
| Situation | First move |
|---|---|
| "Why is X conserved?" | Find the symmetry (space, time, rotation, phase); no symmetry, no law [ch01] |
| "Gravity is not a force?" | Restate as world lines and curvature; local frames only [ch02] |
| "What does quantum mechanics mean?" | Separate formalism from interpretation; list what each gives up [ch03] |
| Black-hole paradox | Name the colliding principles: unitarity, equivalence, locality [ch04] |
| "Dark energy / fine-tuning" | Ask which probes, how independent, what prior [ch05] |
| "Entropy / arrow of time" | Which entropy; closed or open; special past [ch06] |
| Infinite or "impossible" | Which limit: axiom-independent, uncomputable, unsolved [ch08] |
| Any paradox | Name the hidden assumption; classify [ch09] |
| Any claim | Place it on the evidence ladder [ch10] |

## Symmetry and Conservation [ch01]
| Symmetry | Conserved quantity |
|---|---|
| Space translation | Momentum |
| Time translation | Energy (fails when spacetime itself changes) |
| Rotation | Angular momentum |
| Local phase of the wave function (U(1)) | Electric charge; forces the photon field |

## Interpretations of Quantum Mechanics [ch03]
| Interpretation | Adds | Gives up |
|---|---|---|
| Copenhagen | Collapse; wave function as possibility | A story between measurements |
| Pilot wave | Hidden particle positions | Locality |
| Many worlds | Nothing beyond the wave equation | A single observed world |
Bell tests: local hidden variables are excluded. Observation = interaction/entanglement, not mind.

## Black Holes [ch04]
| Concept | One line |
|---|---|
| Trapped surface | Even outward light moves inward; forces geodesic incompleteness |
| No-hair | Mass, spin, charge only; ringdown support is tentative |
| Entropy | About one bit per four Planck areas of horizon |
| Page time | About half the mass evaporated (about 90% of the time) |
| Firewall | Keeps unitarity, breaks equivalence; contested |
| Holography | A precise duality exists in negatively curved models only |

## Cosmology and Constants [ch05]
- Acceleration was inferred from supernova fits, not measured directly.
- Timescape: probably not right; baryon oscillations and microwave background disagree.
- Evolving dark energy: hinted, slightly favoured by one combination, not confirmed.
- Naturalness = improbable under an explicit prior; options: archer peeked or many arrows.

## Entropy and Information [ch06]
| Lens | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Heat | Less useful work available |
| Counting | log of number of microstates |
| Shannon | Information a measurement would reveal |
| Von Neumann | Zero for pure states; positive for parts of entangled wholes |
Landauer: erasing a bit releases at least k T ln 2 of heat; real chips are millions of times above it. Reversible logic avoids the bound but needs reversible hardware.

## Particles and Fields [ch07]
| Family | Spin | Sharing a state | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bosons | Integer | Unlimited | Condensates, force carriers |
| Fermions | Half-integer | One per state | Structure of matter |
Regularisation: finite part of a divergent sum; Casimir force measured; 3D electromagnetic factor 1/120.

## Limits of Formal Systems [ch08]
| Statement | Status |
|---|---|
| Reals are uncountable | Proved |
| Continuum hypothesis | Independent of the standard axioms |
| Halting problem | Undecidable |
| Perfect compression (Kolmogorov complexity) | Uncomputable |
| NP-complete problems have fast algorithms? | Open (P vs NP) |
| Four colour theorem | Proved, computer-assisted |

## Evidence Ladder [ch10]
Measured > proved > well-tested theory > interpretation > hypothesis > speculation; "believed, not proved" is its own box. Use the matching phrase.
