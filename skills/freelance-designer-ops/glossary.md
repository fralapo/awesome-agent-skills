# Glossary

**Awareness before sale** — for an unrecognised niche, educating the market about the need before selling the service (Ch01)
**Batching** — grouping similar tasks into one block to save the energy lost in switching (Ch03)
**Block** — a 1–4 h calendar slot of one activity; typically 4 h, 3–4 h on one design project (Ch03)
**Block + Goal** — pairing every block with a stated outcome in Google Tasks (Ch03)
**Board view** — Notion kanban view grouped by a property such as Status; dragging a card changes it (Ch02)
**Buffer** — gap of about 15 minutes left between events instead of scheduling back-to-back (Ch03)
**Calendars as labels** — one calendar per life/work area, each with its own colour, so the week reads at a glance (Ch03)
**Cash-in date (incasso)** — payment date of an invoice; under a cash-basis regime it decides the income year (Ch01, Ch02)
**Client design project** — a deck, website or template made for a client; a deck takes about a month part-time (Ch01)
**Clients database** — Notion database of current and past clients, related to Projects (Ch02)
**Consultation** — 1–1.5 h session where the client's slides are fixed live, leaving a before/after (Ch01)
**Contacts database** — Notion list of potential collaborators with profession, email, phone and portfolio (Ch02)
**Content database** — single Notion database for all platforms, with calendar, idea and pipeline views (Ch02)
**Content phase** — collecting brief, links and references in one place; the most decisive phase of a design project (Ch04)
**CRM pipeline** — lead statuses Call → Quote → Awaiting reply → Client → Lost, plus Past clients (Ch02)
**Dashboard** — Notion home page grouping Freelancing, Personal branding and Personal areas (Ch02)
**dateBetween** — Notion function measuring the gap between two dates; used in minutes then divided by 60 (Ch02)
**Delegation** — handing fiscal work to an accountant and footage editing to an editor with a set budget (Ch01, Ch04)
**Design tools** — the craft side of the stack: paper/tablet, inspiration sources, layout, web and presentation software (Ch04)
**Discovery call** — first call to uncover audience, occasion, deadline, slide count, assets and hidden scope (Ch01)
**Energy mapping** — placing high-energy work in your best hours and calls or calm work elsewhere (Ch03)
**Energy (property)** — Low/High rating of a project used to decide whether to accept new work (Ch02, Ch03)
**Feedback (status)** — project status meaning the designer is waiting for the client (Ch02)
**Freelance dimension** — the personal shape of the job built from strengths, weaknesses, aspirations, context and skills (Ch01)
**Gantt** — Notion timeline view of projects, zoomed to Quarter to spot overlaps before promising dates (Ch02)
**Generic properties** — one Projects database with properties fitting every service type instead of one database per service (Ch02)
**Good-enough loop breaker** — stop rule for perfectionism once 100% has been given (Ch03)
**Hour package** — block of hours sold "on consumption" (e.g. 10 h) and monitored in the tracker (Ch01, Ch02)
**Hour tracker** — Notion Tracker database logging task, start, end, time and project (Ch02)
**Hours %** — hours done divided by total hours, shown as a percent bar; over 100% means overrun (Ch02)
**Hours done** — rollup summing tracked Time per project (Ch02)
**Hours remaining** — total hours minus hours done (Ch02)
**Inspiration hygiene** — free browsing on Behance/Dribbble, organised Pinterest boards per project, style drawn from websites rather than decks (Ch04)
**Invoices database** — Notion database with issue date, cash-in date, status, automatic and manual amounts (Ch02)
**Learn → Schematise → Apply** — three-step method for building an organisational tool (Ch02)
**Linked view of database** — a filtered view of another database embedded in a page, e.g. a project's notes (Ch02)
**Manual amount** — invoice field for extras such as stamp duty or deposits, added to the rollup amount (Ch02)
**Marca da bollo** — Italian stamp duty that may need adding to invoices manually (Ch01)
**Matryoshka logic** — Notion's structure of pages inside pages, any of which can be a database (Ch02)
**Niche by specialisation** — positioning on one recognised role (e.g. presentation designer) combining design, storytelling and speaking (Ch01)
**Notebook triage** — paper notebook catching micro-tasks, cleared at day end, also recording what was done (Ch03)
**Notes database** — Notion history of meetings and ideas, linked to one or more projects (Ch02)
**Organisation tools** — the business side of the stack: email, project database, calendar, file sharing (Ch04)
**Organise / Design split** — the two jobs in a freelancer's week: administration/coordination and production (Ch01)
**Paper first** — sketching wireframes and storyboards on paper or tablet before any software (Ch04)
**Partita IVA** — Italian VAT number, opened when freelance work becomes regular (Ch01)
**Past clients** — CRM status for former clients worth recontacting in quiet periods (Ch02)
**Pause at the goal** — using slack after an early-met revenue target to recharge and rethink (Ch04)
**Person / Professional / Project** — separation that directs criticism at the project and role, not the person (Ch01)
**Personal-branding document** — written target audience and reasons for creating content, the "why" anchor (Ch02, Ch04)
**Pipeline (content)** — content statuses To write → To design → To record → To edit (Ch02)
**Prestazione occasionale** — Italian occasional-work arrangement used to test freelancing before a VAT number (Ch01)
**Projects database** — the hub of the workspace: active work and archive with status, timing, budget and hours (Ch02)
**Quote phases** — content immersion, style research and revision rounds stated explicitly in a quote (Ch01)
**Recurring event** — calendar event repeating on a fixed rhythm, e.g. content shooting every 2 weeks (Ch03)
**Regime forfettario** — Italian flat-rate regime: 5% start-up rate for 5 years, then 15%, income on cash-in (Ch01)
**Relation** — Notion property connecting two databases, optionally limited to one item (Ch02)
**Repurposing** — publishing the same vertical content across several platforms to multiply reach (Ch04)
**Revenue view** — table filtered to active or done projects with a Sum on Budget (Ch02)
**Ritenuta d'acconto** — Italian withholding-tax receipt used for occasional work (Ch01)
**Rollup** — Notion property aggregating values from a related database, e.g. summing hours (Ch02)
**Schematisation questions** — which databases, which relations, which properties, which views (Ch02)
**Sit-stand desk** — height-adjustable desk; standing helps concentration and online teaching (Ch04)
**Sponsored (checkbox)** — content property keeping brand collaborations distinguishable (Ch02, Ch04)
**Status board** — Projects view grouped by Status with coloured columns (Ch02)
**Three reasons a deck takes a month** — subject immersion, style research, revisions (Ch01)
**To invoice (status)** — completed-group project status flagging work ready for billing (Ch02)
**Total hours** — manual project budget of hours set from budget and available time (Ch02)
**Training** — 2–4 h compact sessions up to multi-day programmes, online or in person (Ch01)
**View** — a way of displaying a database (table, board, timeline, calendar, list, gallery) built to answer one question (Ch02)
**Yearly review** — annual reflection with a wellbeing goal, a revenue goal and one lesson per project (Ch04)
**Yesterday view** — tracker view filtered by End = yesterday, catching hours logged a day late (Ch02, Ch03)
**Yourself as a client** — tracking own content as a project whose client is you (Ch01, Ch02)
