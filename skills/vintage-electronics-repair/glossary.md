# Glossary

**Anti-skating** - small sideways force on a tonearm that counters its tendency to pull toward the centre of the record (ch05)
**Azimuth** - angle of a head gap relative to the tape; wrong azimuth dulls high frequencies and upsets stereo phase (ch03)
**Backup battery** - cell that keeps the clock and configuration of a computer; a flat or leaking one causes odd symptoms (ch04, ch07)
**Bias (tape)** - high-frequency signal added when recording on magnetic tape to reduce distortion (ch03)
**Capstan** - rotating shaft that drives tape at constant speed against the pinch roller (ch03)
**Cold joint** - solder joint with a hairline crack or poor wetting that causes intermittent faults (ch01)
**Cross-talk / ground loop** - hum caused by two ground paths between devices (ch05)
**CRT** - cathode-ray tube; vacuum display tube with a high-voltage supply (ch02, ch06)
**DAT / ADAT / DCC** - digital tape formats: rotating-head audio tape, multi-track tape, and a cassette-sized digital format (ch03)
**Degaussing (demagnetising)** - removing residual magnetism from heads and metal guides (ch03)
**De-emphasis** - playback equalisation that reverses a boost applied during recording (ch05)
**Deinterlacing** - converting interlaced video fields into progressive frames (ch06)
**Dummy load** - resistor bank that stands in for loudspeakers during amplifier tests (ch05)
**ECC memory** - error-correcting memory used in servers (ch07)
**ESR** - equivalent series resistance; a quick indicator of an electrolytic capacitor's health (ch04, ch08)
**Flyback transformer** - high-voltage transformer in a tube display (ch02, ch06)
**Heater / anode supply** - the low-voltage filament supply and the high-voltage supply of a valve circuit (ch02, ch05)
**Idler** - rubber wheel that transmits motion between a motor and a platter or reel (ch03, ch05)
**Inrush current** - brief surge when a supply is switched on (ch04)
**Isolation transformer** - transformer that separates a unit from the mains reference; not the same as a series lamp (ch02)
**Noise reduction (Dolby, dbx and similar)** - encode and decode system that lowers tape hiss; needs a matching decoder (ch03, ch05)
**Pinch roller** - rubber roller that presses tape against the capstan (ch03)
**Potentiometer** - variable resistor used for volume and tone controls; often noisy with age (ch04, ch05)
**RAID** - disks combined for speed or redundancy; not a backup (ch07)
**Series lamp** - incandescent lamp wired in series with the mains to limit fault current at first power-up (ch04)
**SCSI ID** - unique address of a device on a SCSI bus; duplicates cause failures (ch07)
**Stroboscope (strobe)** - flicker lamp or printed pattern showing platter speed (ch05)
**Tantalum capacitor** - small capacitor type not measured the same way as wet electrolytics (ch04)
**TBC** - time-base corrector; steadies video timing so capture devices can lock (ch06)
**Variac** - variable transformer to raise mains voltage gradually; not isolation (ch04)
**VFD** - vacuum fluorescent display; filament and converter faults are common (ch06)
**XY mode** - oscilloscope mode that plots two signals against each other, used for phase and focus checks (ch03, ch05)
**Zamak** - zinc alloy used in older mechanical parts; can crumble with age (ch03)
