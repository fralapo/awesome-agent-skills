# Glossary

**Adolescent versus adult mindset** - performing for approval versus standing for something and tolerating disapproval (ch10)
**Anchoring (prices)** - treating the purchase price or past peak as the reference for a decision (ch01)
**Anticipated memory** - how we picture the future: as memories we expect to have (ch01)
**Antifragile** - gaining from setbacks; used for the principle-based adult mindset (ch10)
**Attachment (patterns)** - early caregiver relationships shaping how adults behave in intimacy; popular typologies are simplified (ch10)
**Autotelic** - done for its own sake; a feature of flow (ch06)
**Backwards law** - chasing the positive is negative, accepting the negative is positive (ch04, ch10)
**Bad-faith arguers** - dodger, twister, wrangler, liar (ch09)
**Basic / complex / intelligent failure** - simple error in known territory; multi-cause breakdown; small result of an experiment in new territory (ch11)
**Bayesian updating** - revising a neutral starting estimate by how well each piece of evidence fits (ch13)
**Choice overload** - many options raise difficulty and deferral; the effect size is contested (ch13)
**Choking** - performance falling under pressure because attention is turned to well-learned steps (ch06)
**Cognitive load** - how much you try to hold in mind at once; lowering it frees attention (ch06)
**Constructed emotion** - view that emotion is a prediction built from body sensations, context and learned concepts; contested (ch03)
**Contested** - claim with active disagreement or replication problems (SKILL.md)
**Corroboration** - confirming a claim with independent sources or with a costly, checkable track record (ch14)
**Craving** - anticipation of the reward that powers a habit loop (ch07)
**Cue / routine / reward** - the three parts of a habit loop (ch07)
**Deep question** - a question about values, beliefs or experiences rather than facts (ch09)
**Decision record** - written question, options, reasons, probabilities and review date, kept to judge the process later (ch13)
**Default mode network** - brain structures active when undistracted, linked to mind-wandering and inner questions (ch07)
**Depth of study** - how far you have read on a question before holding a view; a simple rated scale (ch14)
**Disaster personality** - how you actually behave in an emergency, often unlike expectations (ch02)
**Disposition effect** - selling winners early and holding losers to avoid realising a loss (ch01)
**Duration neglect** - memory of an episode ignores how long it lasted (ch01)
**Elaborative encoding** - making information meaningful by linking it to what you know (ch05)
**Equanimity** - calm acceptance of what is not up to you (ch04)
**Eudaimonia / hedonia** - a life worth living versus pleasure and comfort (ch10)
**Expected value versus typical path** - the average over many similar choices versus the one path an individual takes (ch13)
**Experiencing self / remembering self** - the self that lives moments and the self that keeps the story and decides (ch01)
**Flow** - state of full present attention with effortless performance (ch06)
**Focusing illusion** - overweighting whatever you are thinking about (ch01)
**FOMO** - fear of missing out; urgency and social proof override analysis (ch01)
**Gambler's ruin** - in a fair repeated game against an unlimited opponent, a finite bankroll is ruined in the end (ch13)
**Goodhart's law** - a metric that becomes a target stops measuring what it was meant to measure (ch14)
**Group flow** - team version of flow; claims contested (ch06)
**Head trash** - self-critical, emotional thoughts that fill spare capacity under saturation (ch02)
**High conflict** - self-reinforcing us-versus-them conflict that narrows perception (ch09)
**Illusion of continuity** - expecting to stay the same while looking back at big changes (ch01)
**Intelligent failure** - see basic / complex / intelligent failure (ch11)
**Interoception** - sensing the body's internal state; feeds simple feelings (ch03)
**Junk information** - true but useless content that still activates reward like useful content (ch14)
**Keystone habit** - a habit that changes self-image and spreads to other behaviours (ch07)
**Looping for understanding** - listen, restate, check, then respond (ch09)
**Loss framing** - motivating by picturing what is lost by not acting (ch07)
**Memory palace** - imagined place filled with vivid images to hold a sequence (ch05)
**Misinformation effect** - suggestion or leading wording changing what is remembered (ch05)
**Motivated reasoning** - unconscious shaping of evidence by what we want to be true (ch01)
**Neutral prior** - an explicit starting estimate with no lean, from which evidence moves you (ch13)
**Normalcy bias** - assuming a threatening event is normal (ch02)
**Operational prioritisation** - in saturation, do the next fastest useful task (ch02)
**Paralysis by analysis** - over-attention to steps of a skilled action (ch06)
**Peak-end rule** - remembered quality driven by the most intense moment and the ending (ch01)
**Positional goods** - goods valued for being better than others; spending on them can race without raising standing (ch15)
**Proportionality, personality, panache** - three principles for ethical rhetoric (ch09)
**Ratchet (lifestyle)** - spending that rises to match income, so a raise does not change daily life (ch15)
**Reappraisal** - reinterpreting arousal or a situation (ch04)
**Regret minimisation** - choosing the option you expect to regret least; a useful guide that has biases (ch13)
**Rosy retrospection** - memory edits the past kindly, so "it was better before" is partly an artefact (ch01)
**Scout / soldier mindset** - mapping what is real versus defending a belief (ch01)
**Side-switching** - writing the best case for the other side before debating (ch09)
**Social stress test** - laboratory task of an impromptu speech and mental arithmetic under hostile evaluation (ch04)
**Source decay** - online sources disappear or change, so record date, address and the quote you rely on (ch14)
**Survivorship bias** - judging a path by those who arrived, ignoring those who did not (ch11)
**Task saturation** - more tasks than you can carry with confidence (ch02)
**Understory** - what a conflict is really about beneath allegations (ch09)
**Vicarious agency** - feeling that change is possible by seeing it done elsewhere (ch04)
**What is up to me** - the Stoic split between what you control and what you do not (ch04)
