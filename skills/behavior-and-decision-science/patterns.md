# Patterns

Recurring situation -> move. Each entry: When / Move / Cost / Not when. Chapter in brackets. Entries marked (position) are interpretation, not evidence. None is clinical advice.

## 1. "Was it worth it?" (retrospective judgment)
**When**: judging a holiday, a project, a procedure after the fact.
**Move**: separate the lived experience from the remembered story; note the ending and the peak; ask which self the decision serves. [ch01]
**Cost**: slower, less intuitive verdict.
**Not when**: the experience is still happening (rate it live).

## 2. Designing an ending
**When**: a service, meeting, onboarding or farewell.
**Move**: make the final moments clear and positive; fix the worst peak first; do not hide a bad middle behind a good end. [ch01]
**Cost**: effort on details users may not consciously notice; ethical care.
**Not when**: the end is irrelevant to the outcome.

## 3. Forecasting "I will be happier if..."
**When**: choosing a city, job, purchase.
**Move**: describe an ordinary week there (activities, people), not the headline feature. [ch01]
**Cost**: less exciting storytelling.
**Not when**: the feature will genuinely occupy daily attention.

## 4. A rule or promise for years ahead
**When**: lifelong commitments, long contracts, legacy rules.
**Move**: assume your future self will differ; stay curious, add humility, build review points; be brave about your future capacity. [ch01]
**Cost**: less rigid certainty.
**Not when**: a deliberate irreversible vow.

## 5. "I know I am right" (position)
**When**: a belief is tied to identity or group.
**Move**: ask what you would expect to see if wrong; give your self-worth a base that is not "being right"; practise feeling intrigued, not defensive. [ch01]
**Cost**: social friction in loyalty-driven groups.
**Not when**: you are knowingly advocating.

## 6. Overloaded now
**When**: too many open tasks, an unexpected shock.
**Move**: name saturation, accept you will not finish it all, do the next fastest useful task, repeat; keep capacity estimate minus two for planning. [ch02]
**Cost**: some important work waits.
**Not when**: one task is critical; triage by consequence first.

## 7. Dread on waking, racing body
**When**: unpleasant sensations before any cause is clear.
**Move**: ask what physical cause could explain it (sleep, hunger, water) before building a story; reinterpret arousal as preparation. [ch03, ch04]
**Cost**: may delay noticing a real problem.
**Not when**: symptoms are severe or persistent; seek a clinician.

## 8. Big test, talk or interview
**When**: evaluative pressure on a well-learned skill.
**Move**: practise under realistic conditions (time limits, audience, camera); write down worries first; keep attention off the steps. [ch06]
**Cost**: uncomfortable rehearsal.
**Not when**: a novel task that needs deliberate reasoning.

## 9. Worry about the uncontrollable
**When**: an interview, a decision pending, bad news.
**Move**: two columns: up to me (preparation, intentions, actions) and not up to me (outcome, other people); invest only in the first, prepare equanimity for the second. [ch04]
**Cost**: repeated effort.
**Not when**: the problem can be changed collectively; then act.

## 10. Remembering a list or speech
**When**: names, orders, talk topics.
**Move**: make it meaningful; place absurd vivid images along a familiar route; memorise topics, not words. [ch05]
**Cost**: set-up time; weak for deep understanding.
**Not when**: you need exact wording or comprehension.

## 11. Acting on a vivid memory or witness
**When**: an accusation or a disputed recollection.
**Move**: look for independent corroboration; avoid leading questions; do not rely on confidence or detail. [ch05]
**Cost**: slower resolution.
**Not when**: records already settle it.

## 12. Replace a habit
**When**: a routine you want to change or build.
**Move**: log cue and reward for several days; keep them; swap the routine; give an immediate reward; tell someone who will hold you to it. [ch07]
**Cost**: days of diagnosis.
**Not when**: a medical or addictive condition; seek professional help.

## 13. Phone reaches during idle moments
**When**: boredom triggers device use.
**Move**: tech-free first hour, meals and last hour; bedroom and classroom as device-free zones; occasional longer fasts; count urges in an hour. [ch07]
**Cost**: access and social cost.
**Not when**: the device is needed for work, safety or health.

## 14. Creative block
**When**: a resistant brief.
**Move**: fix a frame (theme and variation), make many rough attempts, write what you know, test a "rule of the field", work alone first. [ch08]
**Cost**: mess and time.
**Not when**: a safe known solution is required.

## 15. Argument turning toxic
**When**: both sides want out but escalate.
**Move**: agree what the dispute is about; loop (listen, restate, check); look for the understory; match emotional energy; return to the agreed topic. [ch09]
**Cost**: patience; no guaranteed agreement.
**Not when**: safety is at risk, or equal standing of persons is being debated.

## 16. Bad-faith opponent
**When**: dodging, twisting, wrangling, flooding.
**Move**: name the move; return to the point; correct the record; ask what they believe; pick one or two representative claims and assume them true to show consequences. [ch09]
**Cost**: slows the exchange.
**Not when**: the person is simply mistaken.

## 17. Group ideation that fails
**When**: meetings dominated by loud voices.
**Move**: individuals generate ideas alone first, then converge; give quiet people autonomy. [ch08, ch09]
**Cost**: more scheduling.
**Not when**: rapid coordination matters more than idea quality.

## 18. Choosing values (position)
**When**: unhappy despite achievement.
**Move**: ask why you want the goal; prefer immediate, controllable values; ask what struggle you are willing to take on. [ch10]
**Cost**: uncomfortable questioning.
**Not when**: acute distress; seek support first.

## 19. Choosing and keeping a partner (position)
**When**: dating or a strained long relationship.
**Move**: treat compatibility as work; ask how each of you struggles; use language that owns your part; do not give up at the first conflict. [ch10]
**Cost**: patience and honesty.
**Not when**: abuse or fear; leave and seek help.

## 20. After a failure
**When**: a project or experiment went wrong.
**Move**: classify (basic, complex, intelligent); check hypothesis and size; code the stakes honestly; design the next smaller experiment. [ch11]
**Cost**: review time.
**Not when**: the harm is serious; then it is an incident, not an experiment.
