# Glossary

**Abstraction (generation)** - distilling experience into reusable building blocks; the first of two processes in the adaptation model (ch02)
**Abstraction vs idealisation** - ignoring present details vs attributing known-false properties to simplify (ch04)
**Actor-critic** - learner with a differentiable value estimate so gradients can flow (ch03)
**AGI (as used in debates)** - contested; either broad human-level skill or efficient adaptation to novelty (ch01)
**Algebraic generalisation** - applying a rule with variables to inputs outside the training space (ch02, ch03)
**Anthropomorphism error** - crediting or denying a model capabilities by assuming human-likeness (ch04)
**Autoregressive** - producing the likely continuation token by token (ch03)
**Batching** - serving many users together so weight fetch is shared (ch07)
**Bottleneck (moving)** - the binding constraint in a given period; changes over time (ch06)
**Chain of thought** - intermediate step text produced before an answer (ch02)
**Chinese room** - thought experiment arguing symbol manipulation alone is not understanding (ch04)
**Collective brain** - the idea that capability comes from connected populations learning socially (ch09)
**Collective intelligence** - the system of people, markets and machines as the unit of intelligence (ch01)
**Complementary input** - a resource that cannot be replaced by automation and so bounds output (ch07, ch08)
**Consciousness-adjacent language** - proposed vocabulary for model behaviour that invites consciousness talk (ch04)
**Conversation illusion** - the impression of a continuing dialogue, produced by resending the whole transcript each turn to a stateless model (ch04)
**Dark matter of AI (knowledge representation)** - phrase for the hidden importance of representation (ch03)
**Deception (search)** - when stepping stones to a goal do not resemble the goal (ch05)
**Dumb superintelligence** - contradictory scenario of a system smarter in all respects yet blind to human goals (ch01)
**Entangled representation** - internal structure with redundant, non-modular pieces despite correct output (ch05)
**Exposure vs adoption** - what tasks a model could do versus what is actually delegated to it (ch10, ch11)
**Extension vs intension** - values of a function vs the rule that generates them (ch02)
**Extrapolation** - continuing a pattern outside the region covered by examples (ch02)
**Falsifier** - an observation that would show a claim false (all chapters)
**Few-atoms-of-meaning hypothesis** - the world is a few atoms of meaning repeated and composed (ch02)
**Fractal intelligence** - informal picture of inconsistent mix of retrieval and reasoning (ch02)
**Generalisation spectrum** - none, local, broad, extreme (ch02)
**Hybrid system** - learned components plus symbolic or search components (ch03)
**In-context learning** - adapting from examples in the prompt; viewed as gradient-descent-like (ch07)
**Intelligence explosion** - feedback loop where AI speeds the research that improves AI (ch07, ch08)
**Interpolation** - predicting between known data points on a learned manifold (ch02)
**KV cache** - stored keys and values of past tokens read at each decoding step (ch07)
**Labour share** - fraction of output paid to labour (ch06)
**Liar's dividend** - the benefit to anyone who can dismiss genuine evidence as fabricated once fakes are common (ch11)
**Lump of labour fallacy** - assuming a fixed amount of work (ch06)
**Manifold hypothesis** - natural data lie on low-dimensional manifolds (ch02)
**Mask / simulator view** - a model as a generator of role-played characters (ch04)
**Mechanism / capability / prediction** - the three levels of a claim (ch10)
**Memorisation game** - benchmark passable by prior memory (ch01, ch03)
**Neurosymbolic** - combining neural networks with symbolic reasoning (ch03)
**Nines (reliability)** - successive 9s of reliability; multiplicative over steps (ch06, ch07)
**Novelty search** - rewarding behaviour different from the archive (ch05)
**Omnidirectional inference** - predicting any subset of variables from any other (ch07)
**Phase gate** - maturity criteria for moving a technology between stages (ch06)
**Platonism objection** - neat hidden reality under messy appearances is an assumption (ch02, ch04)
**Position** - a contested thesis with arguments for and against and a falsifier (all)
**Quality-diversity** - archive of high-quality solutions across behaviour niches (ch05)
**Roofline** - bound step time by the larger of compute and memory time (ch07)
**Shoreline** - the chip edge available for communication (ch06)
**Skill vs generalisation** - performance vs rate of acquiring performance from new experience (ch01)
**Stepping stone** - an intermediate discovery that opens later ones (ch05)
**Superposition** - packing more features than dimensions at the cost of interference (ch07)
**Synthesis (program)** - assembling building blocks into a model of a new situation (ch02)
**System 1 / System 2** - fast intuitive vs slow deliberate processing; mixed in practice (ch02)
**Systems reply** - the whole system, not the part, may understand (ch04)
**Test-time compute** - extra inference-time search or sampling (ch02, ch03)
**Turing-complete (language model)** - question of whether a bounded-context model can compute anything computable (ch03)
**Variants test** - fresh templated variants to separate memory from procedure (ch10)
**Verifier** - a mechanical check of outputs (ch03)
**World model** - learned predictive model of the relevant state of the world (ch03)
