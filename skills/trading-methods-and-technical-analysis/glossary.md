# Glossary

**ADX** - indicator of trend strength, not direction (ch02)
**Airbag** - certificate feature measuring loss from the barrier instead of the strike (ch10)
**Altman Z-Score** - accounting-based score of bankruptcy risk (ch09)
**ATR (average true range)** - average size of a bar including gaps; the instrument's unit of normal movement (ch02, ch06)
**Autocall** - early redemption when underlyings are above a trigger on an observation date (ch10)
**Awesome Oscillator** - 5-period minus 34-period average of the median price, as a histogram (ch02)
**Barrier / knock-out** - level whose touch ends or changes a product's payoff (ch10)
**Beneish M-Score** - accounting-based score flagging possible earnings manipulation (ch09)
**Break-even stop** - stop moved to the entry price after a gain (ch06)
**Break-even win rate** - hit rate at which expectancy is zero, 1 / (1 + R) (ch06)
**Breakout / false break** - close beyond a level / quick return inside after crossing it (ch01, ch05)
**Chikou** - Ichimoku close shifted back 26 periods (ch03)
**Closing stop** - stop that acts only on a close beyond a level (ch06)
**COT report** - weekly report of futures positions by trader category (ch04)
**Commercials / non-commercials** - hedgers / large speculators in the COT report (ch04)
**Contract for difference** - over-the-counter margin contract paying price changes on a notional (ch06, ch10)
**Corridor** - binary certificate repaying a fixed amount if neither of two barriers is touched (ch10)
**Discount certificate** - underlying bought below price in exchange for a capped payoff (ch10)
**Divergence** - price makes a new extreme that the oscillator does not confirm (ch02)
**Doji** - candle whose open and close are about equal (ch03)
**Drawdown** - fall from a peak of equity to a later trough (ch06)
**Engulfing** - second candle whose range engulfs the first (ch03)
**Expectancy** - average profit per trade after costs (ch06, ch08)
**Fixed leverage** - product delivering a set multiple of the daily return, reset each day (ch10)
**Gap premium** - part of a turbo's price paying for gap risk (ch10)
**Heikin Ashi** - smoothed candles built from averaged prices (ch02)
**Hit rate** - share of winning trades (ch06, ch08)
**Ichimoku** - system of midpoint lines and a forward-shifted cloud (ch03)
**Kijun / Tenkan** - 26- / 9-period midpoint lines of Ichimoku (ch03)
**Kumo (cloud)** - area between two Ichimoku lines shifted 26 periods forward (ch03)
**Leverage** - notional controlled divided by capital posted (ch06, ch10)
**Martingale** - adding to a losing position or doubling after losses (ch06)
**Mean reversion** - tendency, assumed by a method, for price to return toward an average (ch05)
**Memory (coupon)** - recovery of missed conditional coupons on a later date (ch10)
**Mini future** - knock-out certificate with a financing-adjusted strike and a residual value (ch10)
**Momentum (cross-sectional)** - holding the strongest performers of a basket over 6-12 months (ch05)
**Notional** - full market value of an exposure, independent of margin (ch06)
**Open interest** - number of open futures contracts (ch04)
**Overfitting** - parameters tuned to noise in past data (ch08)
**Pair trade** - long one related instrument and short another, equal value (ch05)
**Parity** - ratio converting underlying points into a certificate's price (ch10)
**Piotroski F-Score** - nine binary tests of profitability, leverage and efficiency (ch09)
**Pivot points** - levels computed from the prior session's high, low and close (ch01)
**Pullback / retest** - return of price to a broken level or line (ch01)
**Reward-to-risk (R)** - planned gain divided by planned loss (ch06)
**RSI** - relative strength index, ratio of average up-closes to down-closes on a 0-100 scale (ch02)
**Seasonality** - average path of a calendar window over past years (ch04)
**Shareholder yield** - dividends plus net buybacks plus net debt reduction, over price (ch09)
**Stochastic** - position of the close inside the recent high-low range (ch02)
**Support / resistance** - zone where buying / selling previously stopped price (ch01)
**Time decay (theta)** - loss of option value as expiry approaches (ch10)
**Trailing twelve months (TTM)** - sum of the last four quarters (ch09)
**Trendline** - line joining swing points in a trend; its break questions the trend (ch01)
**Turbo** - leveraged certificate whose strike equals its knock-out level (ch10)
**Walk-forward test** - optimise on one window, trade the next, repeat (ch08)
**Worst-of** - payoff set by the weakest underlying of a basket (ch10)
**Wyckoff phases** - accumulation, markup, distribution, markdown (ch01)
