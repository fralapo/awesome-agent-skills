# Glossary

**Accumulation phase** - block of weeks where volume and difficulty rise from conservative to near the limit (ch08)
**Autoregulation** - adjusting sets, loads or reps from short-term feedback (RIR, soreness, bar speed) instead of a fixed plan (ch03, ch07)
**Block periodization** - distinct phases (for example accumulation, intensification, taper) in sequence (ch08)
**CNS fatigue** - claimed central nervous system limit on training; a direct test found post-session fatigue predominantly peripheral after the first day (ch09)
**Concurrent training** - endurance and resistance training in the same programme (ch11)
**Deload** - planned period of reduced training to dissipate fatigue; full week off, taper or standard (ch08)
**Double progression** - add reps within a range, then add load and restart the range (ch07)
**Effective reps** - model claiming only the last few reps before failure produce growth; contested (ch05)
**e1RM (estimated one-rep max)** - estimate of a max from a submaximal set using a formula or chart (ch07)
**Fitness-fatigue model** - training adds both fitness and fatigue; performance is the difference (ch09)
**Functional overreaching** - short-term overload with a performance drop followed by a gain (ch09)
**Hard set** - working set near failure, with good technique, in roughly 5-30 reps (ch03)
**Interference effect** - reduced strength gains when endurance training is combined with lifting (ch11)
**Junk volume** - sets past the point where quality and stimulus fall (ch03)
**Leverage (bodyweight)** - changing the lever arm of the body against the support to tune difficulty (ch06)
**Linear progression** - adding load each session or week; typical for beginners (ch07)
**Maintenance volume (MV)** - dose that holds size without growth (ch03)
**Maximum adaptive volume (MAV)** - productive middle band of volume (ch03)
**Maximum recoverable volume (MRV)** - most volume you can recover from; a moving target (ch03)
**Mesocycle** - training block of roughly 3-8 weeks (ch08)
**Minimum effective volume (MEV)** - lowest volume that produces growth (ch03)
**Mind-muscle connection** - internal attentional focus on the working muscle (ch06)
**Motor unit** - one motor neuron plus the fibres it controls; fires all-or-none (ch02)
**Non-functional overreaching** - longer slump without supercompensation (ch09)
**Novice effect** - almost any harder-than-before stimulus works at first (ch11)
**Overtraining syndrome** - extreme prolonged state; in sports medicine a diagnosis of exclusion; the word is used for several things (ch09)
**Phase potentiation** - one phase of training prepares the next (ch08)
**Progressive overload** - gradual increase in demand over time (ch07)
**Proximity to failure** - how many reps remain before failure (ch05)
**RIR (reps in reserve)** - reps you could still complete; RIR 0 = failure (ch05)
**ROM (range of motion)** - distance a joint moves through during a rep (ch06)
**RPE (rating of perceived exertion)** - 1-10 difficulty scale; RPE 10 = no reps left (ch05, ch07)
**SAID** - specificity of adaptation to imposed demand (ch02, ch06)
**Size principle** - motor units recruit from small to large as demand rises (ch02)
**SRA (stimulus-recovery-adaptation)** - cycle in which adaptation occurs after the stimulus is removed (ch09)
**Specificity** - adaptation is specific to the demand; a spectrum (ch06)
**Taper** - reduce volume while keeping intensity before a peak (ch08)
**Technical failure** - technique breaks down so another good rep is impossible (ch05)
**Two-for-two rule** - raise load if you get two extra reps on the last set for two weeks (ch07)
**Undulating periodization** - variables rise and fall across days or weeks (ch08)
**Variant ladder** - ordered easier-to-harder versions of a movement pattern used to progress without added weight (ch07)
**Volume load (tonnage)** - sets x reps x load; a weaker proxy than hard sets for hypertrophy (ch03)
